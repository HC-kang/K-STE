# Subject: [Team Drawer] Notice of Authentication Server Replacement on 11/21 (Sat) and Advance Tasks for Administrators

Hello, administrators of Team Drawer. This is the Customer Success Team at MoaBit Account.

We will replace the authentication server from 02:00 to 04:00 on 11/21 (Sat). New logins will not be possible during this time.

## Scope of Use During the Work

Users who are logged in before the work begins can view documents. Documents cannot be edited or saved during the work.

## What Administrators Need to Do

1. All administrators who receive this email must confirm their mobile phone number under "Emergency Contact" on the admin screen by 17:00 on 11/19 (Thu).
2. Even if your mobile phone number has not changed, you must click the "Confirm" button. You do not need to re-enter the number.
3. Administrators of companies that have integrated external systems must issue and apply a new authentication key in the integration settings by 18:00 on 11/20 (Fri).

Companies that have not integrated external systems do not need to issue or apply an authentication key.

## Preview Testing of the New Authentication Key

You may test the new authentication key in advance. Testing is optional. The test screen is available daily from 09:00 to 18:00, from 11/18 (Wed) through 11/20 (Fri).

## Changes After the Work

After the work is complete, the storage capacity of 200GB per company will remain unchanged. Fees will not change.
