Starting at 18:05 on 12/03 (Thu), new entry to the online classroom "클래스돛" will not be possible. The cause is a queue backlog on the entry token issuance server.

Instructors and students who entered before 18:05 can continue their classes. Instructors who entered before 18:05 must not leave the room for inspection purposes.

Platform team member 배유찬 is in charge of recovery. The recovery target is 12/03 (Thu) 20:00. Actual recovery updates will be announced in the #수업-운영 channel.

## Class Relocation

- All classes scheduled to start at 19:00 on 12/03 (Thu) must move to the alternate classroom "이어룸."
- For classes scheduled to start at 20:30, the operations team will separately confirm the venue at 20:10.

## Tasks for Instructors of 19:00 Classes

- Find your class code in the shared document "1203 대체교실" and confirm the 이어룸 address.
- Complete the 이어룸 connection check by 18:40.
- You do not need to send the address to students separately. Class operations manager 정다빈 will send the alternate address to students of the 19:00 classes by 18:45.

## Capacity and Splitting

Each 이어룸 room holds 24 people, and the instructor counts toward capacity. Classes with 25 or more people including the instructor must request a split from 정다빈. The deadline for split requests is 12/03 (Thu) 18:25. 정다빈 will enter the additional room address in "1203 대체교실" by 18:35.

## Recording and Attendance

Classes held in 이어룸 must not be recorded. Instructors of 이어룸 classes must record attendance manually and submit it to the shared folder "수업운영/대체출석" by 12/04 (Fri) 10:00.

Classes that started in 클래스돛 before the outage can use the existing automatic attendance records. These classes do not need to submit manual attendance.
