# Cold Raw Material Receiving Hold Runbook (Seorion Food, East Distribution Center)

## Background

A calibration omission was found in the thermometers of Seorion Food vehicles. Because of this, raw materials from the designated vehicles will undergo additional inspection.

## Scope

- Applicable period: 11/16 (Mon) to 11/18 (Wed).
- Target: vehicles arriving at Dock 2 of the East Distribution Center whose license plate ends in 62 or 84.
- Vehicles outside the target keep the existing inspection.
- Vehicles outside the target do not require this additional 3-box measurement or additional hold registration.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Receiving inspector Han Ji-o | Measures temperature. |
| Quality duty officer Jung Da-bin | Decides whether held materials can be used. |
| Warehouse team leader Yu Tae-geon | Assigns storage locations. |

## Procedure

1. When a target vehicle arrives, the center temperatures of 3 raw material boxes must be measured within 15 minutes.
2. All three measured values must be recorded.
3. If all three values are between 0.0°C and 5.0°C inclusive and there is no damage to the box seals, the materials are received normally.
4. If any measured value falls outside the range, all raw materials from that vehicle must be held.
5. Even if the measured values are within range, if box seal damage is found, all raw materials from that vehicle must be held.
6. Held materials must be moved to refrigerator Zone Q and tagged with a yellow hold tag.
7. Within 30 minutes of vehicle arrival, hold registration must be completed in the inventory system "Chango."
8. Photos of the site may or may not be attached when registering the hold.
9. Jung Da-bin must record an approval or return decision in Chango within 60 minutes of hold registration.

## Prohibitions

- Held materials must not be released to production before Jung Da-bin's approval.
- Materials held due to seal damage cannot be released merely by re-measuring.
