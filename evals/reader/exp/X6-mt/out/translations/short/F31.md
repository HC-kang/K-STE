# Night Emergency Shipment Approval Policy Change

## Reason for Change
Approval records for night emergency shipments have repeatedly been omitted.

## Scope
This policy applies to night shipment staff and shipment team leaders. The day shipment team will continue to follow the existing policy.

## Effective Date
Effective from 20:00 on 11/16 (Mon). It applies to emergency shipment requests received after the effective time.

## Changes
- Phone approval is abolished.
- Emergency shipment requests must be approved by the team leader through the "Emergency Shipment" menu in the 'Jikgyeol' system.
- Staff must register approval requests at least 40 minutes before the scheduled vehicle departure time.
- Team leaders must complete approvals at least 15 minutes before the scheduled vehicle departure time.
- Each request must contain 30 boxes or fewer.
- For requests exceeding 30 boxes, staff must split them into batches of 30 or fewer and request approval for each batch separately.

## In Case of System Failure
- Paper emergency shipment forms may be used only during a system failure.
- Staff must obtain the team leader's signature on the paper emergency shipment form before shipping.
- Staff must enter the records within 2 hours after the system is restored.

## Vehicle Number Changes
If only the vehicle number has changed for an already approved request, staff may correct the number. Re-approval is not required in this case.

## Advance Training
Both staff and team leaders must watch the 12-minute video in the 'Jikgyeol' training menu and click the confirmation button by 18:00 on 11/13 (Fri).

## Inquiries
Direct inquiries to Operations Support, Heo Da-on, via the internal messenger channel '야간출고문의' (Night Shipment Inquiries). System failure reports should also be sent through this same channel.
