# B Archive Room Humidity Alarm Response Runbook (Haesol Document Archive)

## Scope

- This runbook is a temporary response procedure, created because humidity in the archive room may rise during the dehumidifier drain pipe replacement work (Batch 4).
- Effective period: 08:00 on 12/15 (Tue) through 20:00 on 12/17 (Thu).
- Applies only to B Archive Room on B1 (basement level 1).
- For the A Archive Room, the existing procedure applies unchanged.

## Personnel

| Role | Name | Responsibility | Contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facilities on-duty staff | Im Na-gyeom | On-site inspection, operating the portable dehumidifier | None |
| Preservation manager | Cha Su-bin | Decision on document relocation | Ext. 8358 |
| Facilities manager | Ryu Do-hyeon | Equipment support | Ext. 8352 |

## Response Procedure

1. When the monitoring screen (Preservation Eye) shows relative humidity of 60% or higher for 10 consecutive minutes, begin the alarm response.
2. The on-duty staff must arrive at the B Archive Room site within 5 minutes of starting the alarm response.
3. The on-duty staff measures humidity with the handheld meter on site.
4. If the reading is 58% or higher, the on-duty staff must operate the portable dehumidifier.
5. If the reading is below 58%, the on-duty staff does not need to operate the dehumidifier. In this case, the staff must ask Ryu Do-hyeon to inspect the monitoring sensor.
6. The on-duty staff who operated the dehumidifier must measure humidity again with the handheld meter after 20 minutes.
7. If the re-measured reading is 58% or higher, the on-duty staff must ask Cha Su-bin to decide on document relocation.
8. If the re-measured reading is below 58%, the on-duty staff may either stop the dehumidifier or keep it running. If it keeps running, it must be stopped before the end of the on-duty shift.

## Dehumidifier Usage Conditions

- The portable dehumidifier must be connected to the dedicated outlet for the B Archive Room.
- A maximum of 2 dehumidifiers may operate at the same time.
- Hallway outlets and extension cords must not be used.

## Document Relocation

- Cha Su-bin decides on document relocation.
- On-duty staff must not relocate documents on their own.

## Records

- The on-duty staff must record the alarm occurrence time, on-site measurements, and actions taken in Preservation Eye within 40 minutes after starting the alarm response.
- The same items must be recorded even if the dehumidifier was not operated.
- Shifts without an alarm do not need to create a separate alarm response record.

## Equipment Return

- Im Na-gyeom must return the temporary equipment by 20:30 on 12/17 (Thu).

## Inquiries

- Facilities inquiries go to Ryu Do-hyeon (ext. 8352).
- Document relocation inquiries go to Cha Su-bin (ext. 8358).
