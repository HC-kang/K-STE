# Notice of Change to Default Sample Storage Period

The default storage period for sample sample materials will be reduced from 60 days to 30 days, starting with actual analysis samples newly received on Monday, November 23.

## Reason for Change

Samples whose storage periods had expired continued to occupy freezer space, creating a problem.

## Scope

This applies to researchers and sample management staff in the Morafit Research Analysis Laboratory. Educational model samples are excluded from this change.

## Effective Date and Changes

The effective date is Monday, November 23. Starting with actual analysis samples received on this day, the default storage period will be 30 days.

Samples received before Monday, November 23 will retain the existing 60-day storage period. These samples do not need to be re-registered under the new rules.

## Procedures for New Samples

1. The receiving researcher must register new samples in the "Input Registration" section of the "Sample Tteul" system by 5:00 PM on the day of receipt.
2. The sample management staff must print labels from the system and attach them to the sample containers by 12:00 PM on the next business day after registration.
3. If a storage extension is needed, the receiving researcher must submit an extension request in "Sample Tteul" by 5:00 PM three days before the expiration date.
4. The Head of the Analysis Laboratory, Yeon Bo-min, must approve the extension request.

## Storage Extension Conditions

Extensions are allowed once per sample, for a maximum of 15 days. Storage periods must not be extended without approval.

## Handling of Expired Samples

The sample management staff must move expired samples to the disposal waiting area on the 2nd floor by 4:00 PM on the next business day after expiration. Researchers do not need to move expired samples themselves.

## Confirmation of Revised Regulations

Researchers and sample management staff must check the confirmation box for the revised regulations in "Sample Tteul" by 5:00 PM on Friday, November 20.

## Inquiries

Direct inquiries to Research Support, Bae So-un, at extension 462.
