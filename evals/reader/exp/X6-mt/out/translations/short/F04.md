# December 8 (Tue) Emergency Evacuation Drill Notice

The company-wide emergency evacuation drill will be held on 12/08 (Tue) from 14:00 to 14:20. The emergency exit of the logistics building has changed, and this drill is intended to help employees become familiar with the revised evacuation route.

## Drill Participants and Time

- The drill will take place on 12/08 (Tue) from 14:00 to 14:20.
- All delivery drivers, sorting staff, and office staff who are on-site that day must participate.

## Drill Procedure

1. When the drill begins, stop all work in progress.
2. Proceed to the changed east emergency exit.
3. Gather in outdoor Parking Lot B.
4. The team leader will check attendance in Parking Lot B after the drill ends.
5. Do not return to work until attendance has been confirmed.

**The west main entrance must not be used during the drill.** The existing assembly point, Parking Lot A, is not being used for this drill because equipment has been stacked there.

## Exemptions from On-Site Drill and Alternative Video

- Drivers who are out on external deliveries at 14:00 on 12/08 (Tue) are not required to take part in the on-site drill. These drivers must watch the evacuation video on the virtual training app "Gilbaeum" by 18:00 on 12/09 (Wed).
- Employees who are on full-day leave on 12/08 (Tue) are not required to take part in either the on-site drill or the alternative video.

The video runs 8 minutes. To complete the training, video recipients must answer all 4 comprehension questions correctly after watching. If any answer is incorrect, they may retake the quiz, with no limit on the number of retakes.

## Team Leader Submission Requirements

Team leaders must submit the list of expected participants to the safety portal by 16:00 on 12/07 (Mon). On the list, team leaders must separately mark employees scheduled for external deliveries and employees on full-day leave.

## Mobility Support Requests

Employees who need mobility assistance may apply to the Safety Team, Jeong Ra-gyeom, extension 518, by 15:00 on 12/04 (Fri). Requesting an escort when applying is optional.

## In Case of an Actual Incident

If an actual fire or accident occurs, the drill must be stopped immediately. In that case, instructions from the on-site announcement regarding actual incident response take priority.

## Inquiries

- For questions about the drill and video completion, contact the Safety Team, Jeong Ra-gyeom, extension 518.
- For questions about errors in list submission, contact the Operations Team, Seo Do-yoon, extension 206.
