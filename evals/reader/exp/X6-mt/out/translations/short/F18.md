# Runbook: Switching to the Backup Line During the 11/27 (Fri) Online Performance

This runbook defines the procedure for the broadcast engineer to switch to the backup line, in case transmission is interrupted by maintenance on the primary line during the online performance on 11/27 (Fri) from 19:30 to 21:00.

## Scope

- The performance runs on 11/27 (Fri) from 19:30 to 21:00.
- The work takes place in the broadcast room on the 3rd floor of the headquarters, and the work is performed in the console "별송."

## Roles

| Role | Name | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Broadcast engineer | Yoon Taerim | Executes the switch to the backup line. |
| Audio engineer | Seo Gaon | Checks the audio after the switch. |
| Site manager | Moon Haesol | Decides whether to stop the performance broadcast. |

## Preparation

1. Yoon Taerim must complete the backup line connection test by 19:00.
2. Yoon Taerim must check the test video only on the internal preview.
3. The test video must not be sent to the viewer channel.
4. The automatic return-to-primary-line function must be disabled for the entire performance.

## Switching Criteria During the Performance

| Primary line status | Action |
|---|---|
| Packet loss of 3% or higher continues for 20 consecutive seconds. | Yoon Taerim must switch to the backup line. No separate approval from Moon Haesol is required. |
| Packet loss is below 3%. | No switch is required. The internal preview may be checked. |
| Packet loss of 3% or higher lasts less than 20 seconds. | No switch is required. The internal preview may be checked. |

## Procedure After Switching

1. Yoon Taerim switches to the backup line.
2. Seo Gaon must record the audio check result in the "별송" work window within 40 seconds from the time the switch is executed.
3. If there is an issue with the audio, Seo Gaon must report it to Moon Haesol immediately.
4. If packet loss of 3% or higher continues for 20 consecutive seconds on the backup line, Yoon Taerim must broadcast the standby screen.
5. Only Moon Haesol decides whether to stop the performance broadcast.

## Prohibited Actions

**Even if the primary line appears normal, do not manually switch back to the primary line before the performance ends.**

## Post-Performance Submission

- Yoon Taerim must submit the switch time and packet loss records by 21:20.
- If no switch occurred, Yoon Taerim must still submit "No switch."
