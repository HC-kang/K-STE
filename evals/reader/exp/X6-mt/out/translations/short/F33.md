# Customer Visit Transportation Expense Policy Change Notice

## Reason for Change

Claims for customer visit transportation expenses have increasingly been rejected because the project code was missing. The policy is therefore being revised.

## Scope

This applies to customer visit transportation expenses for field designers and project leads. Commuting expenses are not covered by this policy change.

## Effective Date and Limits

The change takes effect for usage on 12/01 (Tue). The per-use support limit for customer visit taxi fares will change from 35,000 KRW to 28,000 KRW.

## Registration and Approval

- Employees who use transportation expenses must register the receipt and project code in the "Customer Visit" menu of the "Biyong-it" system by 18:00 on the next business day after the day of use.
- Project leads must complete approval by 15:00 on the next business day after registration.
- Usage by project leads themselves must be approved by Operations Team Lead Do Yeo-ul by the same deadline.

## Equipment Transport Exception

- For visits where equipment is transported at the customer's request, up to 45,000 KRW per trip may be supported.
- Approval must be obtained from Operations Team Lead Do Yeo-ul in the "Biyong-it" system before taking the taxi.
- To apply this exception, the customer's transport request email must also be attached.
- Any amount exceeding the support limit without prior approval will be borne by the individual.

## Claim Method

A single receipt must not be split into multiple claims. Fellow passengers do not need to submit separate claims. Only the person who actually paid may submit a claim.

## Transportation Expenses Used Through 11/30 (Mon)

The existing limit applies to transportation expenses used through 11/30 (Mon). These usages may be registered by 18:00 on 12/03 (Thu). No revision to the new limit is required.

## Advance Preparation

Target employees must set a default project code in their "Biyong-it" profile by 17:00 on 11/27 (Fri).

## Inquiries

Direct inquiries to Finance Support, Sae-ron Min. You can reach them via the messenger "교통비도움" (Transportation Help).
