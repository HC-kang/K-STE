# Visitor QR Issuance Outage (December 17)

Starting at 08:50 on 12/17 (Thu), visitor QR issuance stopped in the headquarters visitor system, "MoonPass." The cause is the expiration of the API key used for the text message integration.

Facilities IT team member Somdam Lee is in charge of recovery. The recovery target is 13:00 on 12/17 (Thu). The actual recovery time will be announced in the #HQ-Visitor-Info channel.

Employee access cards work normally. Visitor QRs issued before the outage can still be used within their validity period. These visitors do not need paper passes. Visitors with a valid QR do not need additional escorts due to this outage.

## Reservation Staff

1. If any visitors scheduled between 09:00 and 12:00 on 12/17 (Thu) do not have a valid QR, the reservation staff must submit a temporary list.
2. The temporary list must be submitted by 09:20 on 12/17 (Thu) using the internal form "1217 Visit Alternative," entering each visitor's name, visit time, and the host employee's extension.
3. Visit reservations before 12:00 added after 09:20 must not be entered on the form. They must be registered by phone with the front desk (extension 107). Registration must be completed at least 20 minutes before the scheduled visit time.
4. Visitors who receive paper passes must be met directly by their host at the 1st floor and escorted.
5. Visit reservations after 12:00 must be handled after checking the follow-up notice from the Operations Team, which will be posted at 11:30 on 12/17 (Thu). No temporary list is required for these reservations at this time.

## Front Desk Staff

1. Paper passes must be issued at the 1st floor front desk of headquarters to visitors who do not have a valid QR. **Paper passes cannot be issued in the 3rd floor reception room.**
2. Issue one paper pass per visitor. Paper passes are valid until 18:00 on the same day.
3. Paper passes must be collected from visitors when they leave.
4. Report the number of paper passes issued and returned to Haeon Choi of the General Affairs Team by 18:20 on 12/17 (Thu).

For visitor phone registrations and pass inquiries, contact the front desk at extension 107. For QR system error inquiries, contact Somdam Lee at extension 733.
