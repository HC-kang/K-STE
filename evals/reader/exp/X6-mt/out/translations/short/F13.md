# Meeting Minutes: Return Equipment Receiving Procedure

## Meeting Information

- Date and time: Monday, November 16, 2026, 08:30–09:00
- Location: West Logistics Center, Training Room
- Agenda: Finalize the return equipment receiving procedure

## Reason for Change

Return equipment and outbound equipment traffic paths overlapped, causing delays in loading.

## Scope and Start Date

- Applies to portable cooling fans returned to the West Logistics Center, starting Wednesday, November 18.
- Does not apply to returns to the East Logistics Center.

## Decisions

### Advance Registration and Reservation

- Collection drivers must register their vehicle number and return quantity in the "Doraon" system by 16:00 on the day before their visit.
- The scheduled return time is 10:00–12:00.
- A single vehicle may carry up to 8 cooling fans.
- The default unloading location is Dock 3 at the West Logistics Center.
- If the return quantity exceeds 8 units, the collection driver must split the load across vehicles and make a reservation for each vehicle.
- Collection drivers must not load excess units onto one vehicle to return them.

### Equipment with Water Leak Traces

- Collection drivers must contact receiving manager Seo Na-gyeom before unloading.
- Equipment with water leak traces must be unloaded at Quarantine Zone Q, not at Dock 3.

### Same-Day Emergency Returns

- Returns without prior system registration are allowed only if approved in advance by dispatch manager Ko Si-on.
- The per-vehicle quantity limit applies to emergency returns as well.

### Photo Registration After Receiving

- Receiving manager Seo Na-gyeom must take two exterior photos of each piece of equipment and attach them to the system.
- Photographing and attaching must be completed by 17:00 on the day of return.

## Procedure Demonstration

- Date and time: Tuesday, November 17, 2026, 15:00–15:30
- Location: Dock 3
- Receiving managers must attend.
- Collection drivers do not need to attend.

## Contact

For reservation changes and system errors, contact dispatch support Ryu Chae-un (ext. 267).
