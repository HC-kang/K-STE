# Year-End Expense Settlement Notice: Early Closing of December Business Expenses

This notice is for all employees at Padoyip Design headquarters and branch offices.

## Background

Because we are migrating the year-end accounting system, we will close December business expenses early. Personal expenses are excluded from settlement.

## Schedule

| Item | Deadline | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General expense submission | Mon 12/21, by 15:00 | Business expenses paid 12/01 (Tue) to 12/18 (Fri) |
| Exception expense pre-registration | Fri 12/18, by 12:00 | Business expenses scheduled for payment 12/19 (Sat) to 12/31 (Thu) |
| Exception expense receipt submission | Thu 12/31, by 17:00 | Pre-registered exception expenses |
| Team lead review (general expenses) | Tue 12/22, by 12:00 | Submitted general expenses |
| Team lead review (exception expenses) | Thu 12/31, by 18:00 | Submitted exception expenses |

## General Expense Submission

Employees who incurred business expenses paid 12/01 (Tue) to 12/18 (Fri) must submit them through the virtual system "Sembagguni" by Mon 12/21, 15:00. The submission location is the same for headquarters and branch offices.

- Submission path: Sembagguni > Expense Settlement > December Early Closing
- Each submitted item requires a receipt and a business purpose.
- For paper receipts, a photo attachment is acceptable. You do not need to separately submit the original.
- For any expense of 300,000 KRW or more per item, you must attach the team lead's prior approval record to the general submission. This includes items of exactly 300,000 KRW.

## Exception Expense Registration and Submission

Business expenses scheduled for payment 12/19 (Sat) to 12/31 (Thu) must be pre-registered in the "Exception Expenses" menu of Sembagguni by Fri 12/18, 12:00.

After actually paying for pre-registered exception expenses, you must submit the receipt and business purpose by Thu 12/31, 17:00. For items requiring prior team lead approval, that approval record must also be submitted.

## If You Have No December Business Expenses

Employees with no December business expenses do not need to submit a zero-amount report. No separate confirmation form is required.

## Team Lead Review

Team leads must approve or reject general expenses by Tue 12/22, 12:00, and exception expenses by Thu 12/31, 18:00.

## Payment

General expenses approved by Tue 12/22, 12:00 will be paid on Thu 12/24. Exception expenses are excluded from the 12/24 (Thu) payment.
