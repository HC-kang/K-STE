Subject: [Sodam Axis Equipment] Free Feed Roller Replacement Notice for Label Printer P26 (Serial Numbers S6100–S6199)

Dear Equipment Manager,

Hello. This is the Service Team at Sodam Axis Equipment.

The feed rollers on P26 label printers with serial numbers S6100–S6199 wear out quickly, which can cause label position errors. Sodam Axis Equipment will replace the feed rollers on these units free of charge. The replacement service fee and round-trip shipping are also free.

**The deadline for replacement applications is 12/03 (Thu) 16:00.**

Companies that own affected units, please apply in the following order:

1. Register the serial number, pickup address, and preferred pickup date under "Roller Replacement" on the service portal by 12/03 (Thu) 16:00.
2. You can choose a preferred pickup date from 12/07 (Mon) to 12/09 (Wed).
3. On the selected day, a designated Sodam Axis Equipment courier will visit between 09:00 and 13:00 to collect the printer.

Please note the following when shipping:

- Companies only need to send the printer body.
- Please keep the power adapter and remaining labels separately at your company.
- The printer body must be packed with cushioning material.
- Using the original box is optional.
- Companies without the original box can request a free packing box when registering under "Roller Replacement."

You can rent a replacement unit for the repair period, if you choose. The rental fee is 8,000 KRW per day.

Sodam Axis Equipment will ship the repaired unit within 3 business days after the business day following the day the printer is received. Business days are Monday through Friday.

Companies that have already had the same roller replaced do not need to submit a replacement application. Instead, submit a work confirmation certificate under "Replacement History" on the service portal by 12/03 (Thu) 16:00.

Contact information is as follows:

- Pickup and rental inquiries: Service Team, Lee Ideun (Baek Ideun), or "Service Inquiry" on the service portal
- Work confirmation certificate inquiries: Repair Records Team, Na Haewon, or "Record Inquiry" on the service portal

Thank you.

Sodam Axis Equipment Service Team
