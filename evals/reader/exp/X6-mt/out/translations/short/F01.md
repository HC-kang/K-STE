# Notice on Switching Work Account Authentication and Registering Gyeolgeumm

Because the support for the existing authentication devices is ending, Nurigyeolje Works is switching work account authentication to the virtual system "Gyeolgeumm."

## Who Must Register

All regular and contract employees must register. External visitors who do not have a personal work account are not subject to this requirement.

## Registration Period

The registration period runs from Monday, 11/09, 09:00 to Thursday, 11/12, 18:00. Employees subject to this requirement must register Gyeolgeumm during this period.

## How to Register

1. Register through the in-house portal under My Account > Gyeolgeumm Registration.
2. Registration must be done with your own work account.
3. You must choose one authentication method: the personal mobile phone app or a company security key. You do not have to register both methods.

## Applying for and Receiving a Security Key

Employees who do not want to use a personal mobile phone must apply for a security key on the in-house portal by 12:00 on Tuesday, 11/10. The company provides one security key per person at no cost.

Security keys can be collected at the Support Office on the 2nd floor of the headquarters from 13:00 to 17:00 on Wednesday, 11/11. Bring your employee ID card when collecting the key.

## Employees on Leave

Employees on leave for the entire registration period do not need to register during this period. These employees must register by 17:00 on the day they return to work.

## Application of New Authentication

The new authentication takes effect from Friday, 11/13, 09:00. Employees who have not registered cannot log in to their work accounts until they complete registration.

## Inquiries

- Registration errors: Contact Information Security Team member Da-on Yu via in-house messenger.
- Security key collection schedule: Contact General Affairs Team member So-yul Bae via in-house messenger.

**Confirmation needed:** Employees who register on the day they return from leave may be unable to log in because it will be after 11/13. The notes do not specify how these employees should register, so this has not been included in the notice.
