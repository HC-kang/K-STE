- Date and time: Saturday, 11/28, 14:00–17:00

## Attendance Confirmation

All customers whose enrollment has been confirmed must select either "Attend" or "Cancel" in the "Attendance Confirmation" section of the course page by Wednesday, 11/25, 18:00.

If you select "Cancel" by Wednesday, 11/25, 18:00, your tuition will be fully refunded. Cancellations made after that time are not eligible for a refund.

## What to Bring

- Attendees must have the reservation number screen ready. A printed copy is not required.
- Crafting materials and tools will be provided on site.
- You may bring your own apron. Those who do not bring an apron can borrow one for free on site.

## Parking

Only those who plan to use parking need to enter their vehicle number in the "Vehicle Registration" section of the course page by Thursday, 11/26, 12:00. Those who do not plan to use parking do not need to register.

Registered vehicles can use the underground parking lot of Danbi Hall for free for up to 4 hours on the day of the class. After 4 hours, a fee of 1,000 KRW per 30 minutes applies.

## Inquiries

- For attendance and refunds, please contact Ha-sol Mun of the Education Operations Team. You can submit an inquiry through "Operations Inquiry" on the course page.
