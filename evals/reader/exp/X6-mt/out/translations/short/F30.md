[Yeoul-dam Space] Relocation Notice for Events Booked in Solmaru Hall, December 14 (Mon) – December 18 (Fri)

Dear Event Organizers,

Hello. This is the Reservation Operations Team of Yeoul-dam Space.

Because we are repairing a ceiling leak in Solmaru Hall, all events booked in Solmaru Hall from December 14 (Mon) to December 18 (Fri) will be moved to Byeolmaru Hall on the 2nd floor of the same building. Your reserved usage times and venue fees will remain unchanged.

## Action Required by 15:00 on December 8 (Tue)

All target companies must select either relocation consent or cancellation in "Event Confirmation" on the reservation portal by 15:00 on December 8 (Tue).

- If you select cancellation by 15:00 on December 8 (Tue), the deposit will be fully refunded.
- If you cancel after 15:00 on December 8 (Tue), the cancellation fee under the existing contract will apply.
- Companies with a final attendance exceeding 60 people must request separate space arrangements from the Reservation Operations Team by 15:00 on December 8 (Tue).
- If final attendance exceeds 60 people, agreeing to the relocation alone will not confirm your space.

## Action Required by 12:00 on December 10 (Thu)

- The maximum capacity of Byeolmaru Hall is 60 people.
- Submit your final attendance and seating arrangement via "Event Confirmation" on the reservation portal by 12:00 on December 10 (Thu).
- If you need additional wireless microphones, you may apply through "Add Equipment" on the reservation portal by 12:00 on December 10 (Thu).

## Equipment

If your existing reservation includes wireless microphones, the same quantity you originally requested will be provided. Requesting additional microphones is optional and costs 20,000 KRW per unit. You do not need to reapply for equipment you have already requested.

## Entry and Move-Out

Entry for event preparation is free from 30 minutes before the reservation start time. Earlier entry is not possible. All move-out must be completed by the reservation end time.

## Inquiries

- Space and cancellation inquiries: Seo Ga-on, Reservation Operations Team, via "Reservation Inquiry" on the reservation portal
- Equipment inquiries: Oh Rubin, On-site Support Team, via "Equipment Inquiry" on the reservation portal

We apologize for the inconvenience. We will do our best to ensure your event proceeds smoothly.
