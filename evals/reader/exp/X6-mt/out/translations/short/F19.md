# Nuridam Rental Runbook for Handling Duplicate Exchange Orders

## Background

A retry error in the customer app caused duplicate exchange orders to be created for the same product. This document is item 4 of Bundle 4.

## Scope

- This applies to exchange intake agents at the Nuridam Rental customer service center.
- The effective period is 09:00 on 12/03 (Thu) to 18:00 on 12/04 (Fri).
- Agents process orders in the order system "Damjumun."

## Determining Duplicate Review Targets

If two or more exchange orders with the same contract number, the same product number, and the same collection address are created within 30 minutes, they are subject to duplicate review.

If any one of the contract number, product number, or collection address differs, maintain standard intake.

Orders created more than 30 minutes apart are not eligible for this duplicate cancellation procedure. Agents must not cancel such orders on their own and must maintain standard intake.

## Processing Procedure

1. First, check the shipping status of the orders under review.
2. Take action according to the shipping status, as shown in the table below.
3. Complete the agent cancellation or logistics transfer within 20 minutes of discovering a review target.
4. There is no need to call the customer before cancelling.
5. A notification text message to the customer must be sent after cancellation or transfer.
6. Record the retained order number or logistics transfer number in the order memo.

| Shipping Status | Action |
|---|---|
| All orders under review are "Before shipment" | Retain the first order and be sure to cancel the remaining orders. |
| One or more orders under review are "Shipped" | **Agents must not cancel the orders.** They must be transferred to logistics manager Ko Doyun. |

## Cancellation Reason Codes

- Reason code D14 must be used for duplicate cancellations.
- **Customer change-of-mind cancellation code D41 must not be used.**

## Customer Notification

If the customer requests it, cancellation details may be additionally sent by email. Regardless of whether an email is sent, a notification text message must be sent after processing.
