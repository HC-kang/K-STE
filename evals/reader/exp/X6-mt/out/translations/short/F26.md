Subject: [Nurigyeol Temperature-Controlled Logistics] Notice of Delivery Time Change for Shipments from Dongbu Cold Storage Center, November 12 (Thu) – November 14 (Sat)

Hello. This is the Delivery Operations Team of Nurigyeol Temperature-Controlled Logistics.

Due to floor repairs at the Dongbu Cold Storage Center, delivery times for shipments dispatched from November 12 (Thu) through November 14 (Sat) will change. During the affected period, delivery times will be adjusted from the existing 07:00–09:00 to 10:00–12:00.

This notice applies to food stores receiving shipments from the Dongbu Cold Storage Center.

All affected store owners must submit their receipt availability through the "Delivery Change Confirmation" section of the customer portal by November 10 (Tue), 18:00. Stores unable to receive deliveries between 10:00 and 12:00 must select pickup at a nearby partner cold storage facility on the same submission screen. Stores that select a cold storage facility will visit to collect their goods between 13:00 and 17:00 on the day of delivery. We will cover the storage fees.

Shipments from the Seobu Cold Storage Center will keep their existing delivery times. Stores receiving shipments from the Seobu Cold Storage Center do not need to submit a Delivery Change Confirmation.

During the change period, the upper limit of delivery weight per order remains at 40 kg. There will be no additional delivery charges. Starting with shipments dispatched on November 16 (Mon), the regular 07:00–09:00 delivery schedule will resume.

For questions about selecting a pickup location, please contact Delivery Operations Team representative Ko Se-rin or use the "Delivery Inquiry" section of the customer portal. Order quantity changes can be made on the existing purchase order screen.

Thank you.

Nurigyeol Temperature-Controlled Logistics, Delivery Operations Team
