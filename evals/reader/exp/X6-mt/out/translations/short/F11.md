# Packaging Material Trial Operation Meeting Minutes

## Meeting Overview

- Date and time: Tuesday, November 3, 2026, 10:00–10:30
- Location: 2nd Floor Conference Room, Headquarters
- Agenda: Finalize the packaging material trial operation plan
- Audience: Production Technology Team, Packaging Line 2 Team Leader, Sae-ram Yoon and Ha-jun Do

## Reason and Scope of the Trial

Reports of corner damage have increased for products using the existing cushioning material. Therefore, a new cushioning material will be tested.

- Date and time: Thursday, November 5, 2026, 09:00–12:00
- Location: Plant 1, Packaging Line 2
- Target: 240 boxes using the new cushioning material

## Responsibilities by Person

- Sae-ram Yoon (Production Technology) must place 300 new cushioning materials at the Packaging Line 2 material rack by Wednesday, November 4, 2026, 16:00.
- Ha-jun Do (Line 2 Team Leader) must check the corner condition of all 240 trial boxes.
- Ha-jun Do (Line 2 Team Leader) must register the inspection results in the "Packaging Results" system by Thursday, November 5, 2026, 15:00.

## Trial Suspension and Criteria for Additional Trials

- If 5 or more boxes with corner damage are found, the trial must be stopped immediately.
- If the trial is stopped, Ha-jun Do must report to Sae-ram Yoon by phone.
- If 4 or fewer boxes have corner damage, the scheduled 240-box trial continues.
- Additional trials may only be conducted after approval from Sae-ram Yoon.

## Packaging Line 1

Packaging Line 1 is excluded from this trial. Packaging Line 1 does not need to receive the new cushioning material, and it does not need to register trial results.

## Observation

The Production Technology Team may observe the trial on Thursday, November 5, 2026, 09:00–10:00. Observation is optional.

## Trial Results Review Meeting

- Date and time: Friday, November 6, 2026, 11:00
- Location: 2nd Floor Conference Room, Headquarters
- Attendance: Sae-ram Yoon and Ha-jun Do must attend.

## Inquiries

Inquiries about material shortages and system registration should be directed to Da-on Min, Operations Support (ext. 431).
