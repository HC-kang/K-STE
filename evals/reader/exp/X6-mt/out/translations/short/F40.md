Subject: [Gowunmaru Textile] Request to Coordinate Year-End Fabric Inventory Audit Schedule (Registration Deadline: 12/18 (Fri) 16:00)

To the Inventory Managers of Each Warehouse,

We are re-coordinating the schedule for the year-end fabric inventory audit by warehouse, led by Soo-an Bae of the Finance & Management Team. Due to increased shipments at year-end, the previously scheduled audit on 12/22 (Tue) has been canceled, and we are rescheduling it to times that do not overlap with shipping operations.

**Candidate Schedules**

- 12/23 (Wed) 08:00–10:00
- 12/24 (Thu) 15:00–17:00

Each warehouse will be assigned one of the candidate sessions.

**Requests**

1. Inventory managers must register all available candidate sessions in the inventory settlement system by 12/18 (Fri) 16:00.
2. If both candidate sessions overlap with shipping operations, submit your shipping schedule by email to Soo-an Bae by the same deadline. In this case, you may skip registering candidate sessions.
3. Soo-an Bae will send the confirmed session for each warehouse by 12/21 (Mon) 10:00.
4. Managers on vacation may designate a substitute from the same warehouse. In this case, reply by email to Soo-an Bae with the substitute's name by 12/21 (Mon) 12:00.
5. Result sheets must be uploaded to the inventory settlement system by 18:00 on the day of the audit.

**Attendance**

In principle, the inventory manager must attend in person. Substitutes may be designated only for those on vacation. The audit will be conducted at each assigned warehouse, so there is no need to visit headquarters.

**Inventory Movement Halt**

Each warehouse must halt inventory movement within its warehouse from 30 minutes before the start of the assigned session until its end. Inventory movement outside the session times is permitted.

**Audit Method**

The audit unit is the number of fabric rolls. Packaging does not need to be opened. Rolls whose barcodes cannot be read may be recorded manually.
