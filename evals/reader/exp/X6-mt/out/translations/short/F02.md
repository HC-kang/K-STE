The cafeteria will be closed from Monday, 11/16, through Friday, 11/20, while we replace the drainage pipes. The cafeteria will reopen on Monday, 11/23, at 11:30.

## Lunch Box Orders

All production and office staff who come to work during the closure may order a replacement lunch box. Ordering is optional.

Staff who need a lunch box must order it for each day they need it. Please select your dates in the in-house app "Bapmaru" by 16:00 on Thursday, 11/12.

- Each employee may order one meal per workday.
- Lunch boxes cannot be ordered for days off.
- You must choose one type: regular or vegetarian. You cannot order both types at the same time.
- The employee contribution is 2,000 KRW per meal, regardless of type.

## Lunch Box Pickup

Lunch boxes are collected on the day ordered at the temporary serving counter on the 1st floor of the factory.

- Day shift workers pick up their boxes from 11:30 to 12:30.
- Night shift workers pick up their boxes from 20:00 to 21:00.

## Employees with Food Allergies

Employees who cannot eat either the regular or the vegetarian meal because of a food allergy must report it to the Welfare Team by 16:00 on Thursday, 11/12. Employees who report an allergy may receive a meal allowance without ordering a lunch box.

The meal allowance is 5,000 KRW for each day actually worked from Monday, 11/16, through Friday, 11/20. To receive it, submit your purchase receipt in Bapmaru by 12:00 on Monday, 11/23.

## Canceling an Order

You can cancel a lunch box order in Bapmaru until 12:00 on Friday, 11/13. **After this time, orders cannot be canceled, and the contribution will not be refunded.**

## Inquiries

For questions about lunch box pickup and allergy reports, please contact Ha-rin Ko of the Welfare Team (ext. 462).
