# November Shared Equipment Asset Inspection Notice

Byeolgori Research Institute will conduct a shared equipment asset inspection in November to resolve discrepancies between the equipment management ledger and the actual storage locations. All research and administrative staff are subject to this inspection.

## Inspection Scope

Laptops, tablets, and measuring instruments with company asset numbers are subject to inspection. Personally owned equipment and consumables without asset numbers are not included.

## All Staff

- All staff must review the list of equipment assigned to them in the virtual asset system "Byeoljangbu" and mark it "Confirmed" by 17:00 on 11/24 (Tue).
- Staff with no assigned equipment must mark "No Assignment" by the same deadline.
- Staff with no assigned equipment do not need to submit a separate email report.

## Equipment Holders

Equipment holders must enter the asset number and current location of each piece of equipment in Byeoljangbu. Holders must also attach one photo showing the asset number.

Required actions depend on the equipment's condition:

| Equipment Condition | Required Action |
|---|---|
| Working normally | Only online confirmation in Byeoljangbu is required. You do not need to bring the equipment to the inspection site. |
| Malfunctioning | In addition to online confirmation, return the equipment to the Inspection Office on the 1st floor of the Research Building between 10:00 and 16:00 on 11/25 (Wed). |
| Sent to an external repair vendor | You do not need to return the equipment in person. Attach the repair receipt to Byeoljangbu by 17:00 on 11/24 (Tue). The repair receipt may be used in place of the asset number photo. |

## Additional Verification by Team Leaders

If an individual has three or more pieces of equipment assigned, the team leader must perform additional verification. Team leaders must approve in Byeoljangbu by 12:00 on 11/26 (Thu).

## Discovery of Unregistered Equipment

If you find unregistered equipment during the inspection, report it to the Asset Team regardless of whether it has an asset number. Staff must not dispose of unregistered equipment on their own or transfer it to another person.

## Online Entry Briefing

- Date and time: 15:00–15:30 on 11/20 (Fri)
- Location: internal video conference room "Byeolgyosil"
- Attendance is optional. Skipping the briefing does not waive the inspection obligation.
