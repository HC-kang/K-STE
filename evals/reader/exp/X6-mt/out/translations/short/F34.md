# Walkie-Talkie Rental and Return Policy Change Notice

## Reason for the Change

Walkie-talkie batteries have run low, and returns have been missed, which delayed guidance work at the next performance. The walkie-talkie rental and return policy is therefore being changed.

## Scope

- Applies to venue guidance staff and on-site supervisors.
- External rental company staff use their own equipment. This rental policy therefore does not apply to external rental company staff.

## Effective Date

Takes effect from shifts starting Monday, 12/07. Verbal rentals are discontinued. Walkie-talkie rentals and returns must be recorded in the "Mudae-On" system.

## Guidance Staff Procedure

1. Collect a walkie-talkie from the 1st-floor equipment room using your own employee ID card, no later than 20 minutes before your shift starts. Proxy collection is not allowed.
2. The standard rental quantity is 1 walkie-talkie per person.
3. When collecting, confirm that the battery is at 80% or higher.
4. If the battery is below 80%, request a replacement from the equipment manager. Also enter the reason for the replacement in the rental record in "Mudae-On."
5. When your shift ends, return the walkie-talkie to the charging station in the 1st-floor equipment room within 15 minutes. Then record the return as complete in "Mudae-On."

## Consecutive Shifts

If performances are back-to-back and the gap between two shifts is 30 minutes or less, guidance staff may continue using the same walkie-talkie after the on-site supervisor approves it in "Mudae-On." In this case, it is not necessary to return it in between. When the final shift ends, it must be returned within 15 minutes.

## On-Site Supervisor Procedure

- On-site supervisors may rent 2 additional spare walkie-talkies per performance.
- After the last performance of the day ends, check the list of unreturned items within 40 minutes.
- On-site supervisors must send the list of unreturned items to equipment manager Ha Nu-gyeom.

## Advance Verification

All staff subject to this policy must confirm their employee ID card number in "Mudae-On" by 16:00 on Friday, 12/04.
