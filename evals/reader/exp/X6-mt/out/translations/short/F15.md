# Puren Gyeol Hotel Room Temperature Sensor Replacement Meeting Minutes

## Meeting Overview

- Date and time: Monday, December 14, 2026, 09:00 to 09:30
- Location: Hotel basement operations meeting room
- Agenda: Confirm the sequence for replacing room temperature sensors

## Reason for Replacement and Target Rooms

Repeated discrepancies have occurred between displayed temperature values and actual measured values in guest rooms on the 7th floor. For this reason, the sensors will be replaced.

The target rooms are 12 rooms on the 7th floor, Rooms 701 through 712. Guest rooms on the 8th floor are excluded from this work. No access preparation is required for 8th-floor rooms.

## Responsibilities and Schedule

- Jo Hae-rin (Room Management Team Leader) must register the occupancy and checkout status of target rooms in the "Room-Ieum" work schedule by Tuesday, December 15, 18:00.
- Lee Do-ha (Facilities Manager) will replace the sensors in target rooms from Wednesday, December 16, 10:00 to 14:00. One new sensor is used per room.
- Im Seo-woo (Facilities Duty Staff) must verify each room with a sensor replacement for 20 minutes using a handheld meter.
- Jo Hae-rin may resume sales for rooms that pass verification starting Wednesday, December 16, 16:00.

## Work Sequence

Work begins with vacant rooms as of Wednesday, December 16, 10:00. Occupied rooms may be worked on only with the guest's consent to enter.

Rooms without guest consent to enter are excluded from the day's work. Jo Hae-rin must mark these rooms as "separate schedule required" by Wednesday, December 16, 15:00.

## Verification and Resumption of Sales

**Sales must not be resumed before verification.**

Im Seo-woo makes the pass/fail judgment based on the difference between displayed and measured values as follows:

| Difference between displayed and measured values | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1.5°C or less | Im Seo-woo records as passed. |
| More than 1.5°C | Sales hold remains in place. Lee Do-ha must re-inspect. |

Even for rooms that pass verification, resuming sales on the same day is not required.

## Inquiries

Direct questions about Room-Ieum entries and guest consent records to Operations Support, Bae Ga-on (extension 318).
