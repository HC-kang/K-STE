**Subject: [Sodam Industrial Equipment] Free Replacement of Feed Roller for Label Printer P26 (Serial Nos. S6100–S6199)**

Hello, this is the Service Team at Sodam Industrial Equipment.

We have confirmed that the feed rollers in some Label Printer P26 units, specifically those with serial numbers S6100–S6199, may wear out early and cause label position errors. We will replace the rollers in these units free of charge. Please review the information below and submit your request.

**1. Eligibility**
- Customers who own Label Printer P26 units with serial numbers S6100–S6199

**2. Replacement Request (Required)**
- Deadline: Thursday, 12/03, 16:00
- How to apply: Register the serial number, pickup address, and preferred pickup date on the service portal under "Roller Replacement"
- Available pickup dates: Monday, 12/07 to Wednesday, 12/09
- Pickup time: Our designated courier will visit on the date you select, between 09:00 and 13:00.

**3. Packaging and Shipping**
- Please ship only the printer body. Keep the power adapter and remaining labels separate and store them at your facility.
- Cushioned packaging is required. Using the original box is optional.
- If you do not have a box, you can request a free packaging box when you register for replacement.

**4. Costs and Replacement Equipment**
- Replacement service and round-trip shipping are free of charge.
- You may choose to rent replacement equipment at a rate of 8,000 KRW per day.

**5. If You Have Already Replaced the Same Roller**
- You do not need to submit a replacement request.
- However, you must submit a work confirmation certificate under "Replacement History" on the service portal by Thursday, 12/03, 16:00.

**6. Shipping Schedule for Repaired Units**
- We will ship repaired units within 3 business days after the day they are received. (Business days: Monday to Friday)

**7. Inquiries**
- Pickup and rental inquiries: Service Team, LeeIn-deun / "Service Inquiry" on the service portal
- Work confirmation certificate inquiries: Repair Records Team, Na Hae-won / "Records Inquiry" on the service portal

We apologize for the inconvenience and will do our best to process your request promptly.

Thank you.

Sodam Industrial Equipment Service Team
