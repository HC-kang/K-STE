# Sandbeol Studio Online Concert: Standby Line Switchover Runbook

**Batch 4 / On-Site Use**

## 1. Overview
- **Reason:** Maintenance on the main line equipment may cause a broadcast drop during the show, so a standby line is being prepared.
- **Applicable show:** Fri 11/27, 19:30–21:00
- **Work location:** 3rd floor broadcast room, HQ, console **'Byeolsong'**

## 2. Roles
| Role | Owner | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Broadcast engineer (lead) | Yoon Taerim | Test standby line connection, execute line switchover, broadcast standby screen, submit records |
| Audio engineer | Seo Gaon | Verify audio after switchover and record results |
| On-site manager | Moon Haesol | Decide whether to halt the show broadcast |

**Contacts:** Line – Moon Haesol, ext. **6134** / Audio – Seo Gaon, ext. **6139**

## 3. Timeline
| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| ~19:00 | Yoon Taerim: Standby line connection test complete |
| 19:30–21:00 | Show; monitor main line loss rate |
| By 21:20 | Yoon Taerim: Submit switchover time and loss rate records |

## 4. Pre-Show Preparation (by 19:00)
1. Yoon Taerim completes the standby line connection test.
2. Check the test video **by internal preview only.** **No broadcasting to the viewer channel.**
3. **Disable the main line auto-return function.** It must remain disabled for the entire show.

## 5. Switchover Criteria During the Show (Main Line)
| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Loss rate **at or above 3% sustained for 20 consecutive seconds** | **Switchover to standby line is mandatory.** No separate approval from Moon Haesol required |
| Loss rate below 3% | No switchover needed (internal preview check is permitted) |
| At or above 3% but sustained for less than 20 seconds | No switchover needed (internal preview check is permitted) |

## 6. Post-Switchover Procedure
1. **Yoon Taerim** switches to the standby line and records the switchover time and loss rate.
2. **Seo Gaon** verifies audio within **40 seconds** of switchover and **records the result in the Byeolsong work window.**
3. If an audio problem is found, Seo Gaon **reports immediately to Moon Haesol.**

## 7. If the Standby Line Is Also Faulty
- If the standby line sustains a loss rate **at or above 3% for 20 consecutive seconds**, Yoon Taerim **broadcasts the standby screen.**
- **Only Moon Haesol decides whether to halt the show broadcast.** Yoon Taerim and Seo Gaon must not halt it on their own.

## 8. Prohibited Actions
- Manually switching back before the show ends, even if the main line appears normal
- Enabling the auto-return function
- Broadcasting the test video to the viewer channel

## 9. Post-Show Submission
- Yoon Taerim submits the switchover time and loss rate records **by 21:20.**
- If no switchover occurred, submit **"No switchover."**

## 10. On-Site Checklist
- [ ] Standby line connection test complete before 19:00
- [ ] Test video checked by internal preview only
- [ ] Auto-return disabled and confirmed
- [ ] If switchover occurs, record time and loss rate
- [ ] Seo Gaon's audio verification recorded within 40 seconds
- [ ] Records or "No switchover" submitted by 21:20
