**[Notice] Reduction of Analysis Sample Storage Period and Changes to Management Procedures**

**To:** Researchers and sample management staff, Morapit Research Analysis Lab
**From:** Research Support
**Effective date:** Monday, November 23

---

**1. Reason for Change**
Expired samples have continued to occupy freezer space, so we are changing the storage period and management procedures as follows.

**2. Scope**
- Analysis lab researchers and sample management staff
- Educational model samples are excluded from this change.

**3. Key Changes**

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Default storage period | 60 days → **30 days** |
| Applicable samples | Actual analysis samples received on or after 11/23 (Mon) |
| Existing samples | Samples received before 11/23 (Mon) keep the existing 60-day period (no re-registration required) |

**4. Procedures by Task**

**① Registering new samples (receiving researcher)**
- Location: Sample-tteul system > Receipt registration
- Deadline: By 17:00 on the day of receipt (mandatory)

**② Printing and attaching labels (sample management staff)**
- Print the system label and attach it to the sample container
- Deadline: By 12:00 on the next working day after registration (mandatory)

**③ Extending storage (receiving researcher)**
- Submit the request in Sample-tteul: by 17:00 three days before the expiry date
- Approval from the Analysis Lab Director, Yeon Bo-min, is required
- Extension is allowed once per sample, for up to 15 days
- Extending storage periods without approval is prohibited.

**④ Handling expired samples (sample management staff)**
- Move the sample to the disposal waiting box on the 2nd floor by 16:00 on the next working day after expiry (mandatory)
- Researchers do not need to move the samples themselves.

**5. Confirming the Changed Regulations**
- Who: All researchers and sample management staff
- How: Check the box confirming the changed regulations in Sample-tteul
- Deadline: **By 17:00 on Friday, November 20** (mandatory)

**6. Inquiries**
Research Support, Bae So-un, ext. 462

---

Note that samples received before 11/23 (60 days) and samples received after (30 days) will be stored together for a while, so please check the expiry date on each sample label. This sentence is supplementary guidance that was not in the original memo, so you may delete it if it isn't needed.
