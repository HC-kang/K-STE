## 1. Background

At the previous training session, the start of hands-on practice was delayed due to differences in the settings of trainees' personal laptops. To prevent this, the practical training environment operation method will be changed from this session onward, as described below.

## 3. Decisions

### 3.1 Practice Equipment Operation
- Practice will be conducted on **rental laptops**.
- Use of personal laptops is **in principle not permitted**.
- Exception: Only for trainees who require assistive accessibility programs, and only with **prior approval** from the operations manager **Cha Yeo-jin**, may personal laptops be used.

### 3.2 Supplementary Practice
- Supplementary practice that runs without external network access may be **added at the instructor's discretion**.
- Even with additional practice included, the **training end time remains 16:00**.

### 3.3 Textbooks
- Distribution of printed textbooks is omitted. Neither instructors nor operations staff need to print textbooks.
- Trainees will use the **BaeumKan e-textbook**.
