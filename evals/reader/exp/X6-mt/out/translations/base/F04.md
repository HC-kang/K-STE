**[Company-Wide Notice] Emergency Evacuation Drill on Tuesday, 12/08**

To all employees of Onsaebyeok Logistics,

Due to the change in the emergency exit of the logistics building, we will hold an emergency evacuation drill as follows so that everyone can become familiar with the new evacuation route. Delivery drivers, sorting staff, and office staff must all review the details below.

**1. Drill Date and Time**
- Tuesday, 12/08, 14:00 to 14:20
- All delivery drivers, sorting staff, and office staff on site during this time must participate.

**2. Drill Procedure**
- When the drill begins, stop your work and move to the changed **east emergency exit**.
- The assembly point is **Outdoor Parking Lot B**.
- **The west main entrance cannot be used during the drill.**
- Parking Lot A, the previous assembly point, will not be used for this drill because equipment is stored there. Please gather at Parking Lot B.

**3. Exemptions**
- **Drivers making external deliveries at 14:00**: Exempt from the on-site drill. Instead, you must watch the evacuation video on the virtual training app "Gilbaeum" by 18:00 on Wednesday, 12/09.
- **Employees on full-day leave on Tuesday, 12/08**: Exempt from both the on-site drill and the substitute video.

**4. Video Completion Criteria (for those required to watch the video)**
- After watching the 8-minute video, you must answer all 4 confirmation questions correctly to complete the requirement.
- If you answer any incorrectly, you may retake the quiz, with no limit on the number of retakes.

**5. Instructions for Team Leaders**
- Submit the list of employees expected to participate in the drill to the **Safety Portal** by 16:00 on Monday, 12/07.
- On the list, separately mark employees who are scheduled for external deliveries and those on full-day leave.
- After the on-site drill ends, confirm the headcount of your team at Parking Lot B.

**6. Employees Requiring Mobility Assistance**
- Apply to Jeong Ra-gyeom of the Safety Team (ext. 518) by 15:00 on Friday, 12/04.
- Requesting an escort is optional.

**7. Important Notes**
- If an actual fire or accident occurs during the drill, stop the drill immediately and follow the instructions for actual incident response given over the site announcement system first.
- You may not return to work until your team leader has completed the headcount after the drill ends.

**8. Contacts**
- Questions about the drill or video completion: Jeong Ra-gyeom, Safety Team (ext. 518)
- Errors in submitting the list: Seo Do-yun, Operations Team (ext. 206)

This drill is for the safety of all employees. Thank you for your active participation.

Thank you.
