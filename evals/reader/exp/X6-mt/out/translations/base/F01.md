**Subject: [Mandatory] Notice on Switching Work Account Authentication and Registration of "Gyeol-jam" (Nov 9–12)**

Dear employees,

Because support for our existing authentication devices is ending, we will switch work account authentication to the virtual system **"Gyeol-jam."** The new authentication takes effect on **Friday, November 13, at 09:00**. Before then, please make sure to complete registration during the period below.

**1. Eligibility**
- All regular and contract employees
- External visitors without a personal work account are excluded

**2. Registration Period**
- Monday, November 9, 09:00 to Thursday, November 12, 18:00
- Eligible employees must register.

**3. How to Register**
- Location: Company portal > My Account > Gyeol-jam Registration
- Please register using your own work account.
- Choose **one** authentication method: your personal mobile phone app or a company security key. You do not need to register both.

**4. Security Key Request and Pickup**
If you do not wish to use your personal mobile phone, please request a security key. One key per person is provided free of charge.
- Request: Submit through the company portal by 12:00 on Tuesday, November 10 (required)
- Pickup: Wednesday, November 11, 13:00–17:00, Support Office, 2nd floor, Headquarters
- Please **bring your employee ID badge** when picking up the key.

**5. Employees on Leave**
- Employees on leave for the entire registration period are exempt from this registration.
- However, they must complete registration by **17:00 on the day they return from leave**.

**6. Application of New Authentication and Notes**
- Effective date and time: Friday, November 13, 09:00
- If you have not completed registration, you will not be able to log in to your work account until you do. Please complete it within the registration period so your work is not disrupted.

**7. Contacts**
- Registration errors: Information Security Team, Yu Da-on (company messenger)
- Security key pickup schedule: General Affairs Team, Bae So-yul (company messenger)

Thank you.
