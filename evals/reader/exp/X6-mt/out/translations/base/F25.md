# PR #964: Change Email Notification Resend Interval for Sensor Disconnections

**Company:** Daon Sensor | **Batch:** 5-5 | **Repository:** daon/alerts

## Background of Change
Repeated brief communication disconnections are increasing customer support inquiries due to accumulated alerts for the same equipment. To reduce this, we are changing the resend interval.

## Description of Change
When a disconnection of the same sensor continues:
- The initial email is sent immediately.
- Subsequent emails are resent at 20-minute intervals.
- Connection restoration emails are sent immediately without waiting.

## Scope and Exclusions
| Category | Applicable |
|---|---|
| Standard plan email disconnection alerts | **Applied** |
| Enterprise plan | Excluded (existing behavior maintained) |
| SMS alerts | Excluded (existing behavior maintained) |
