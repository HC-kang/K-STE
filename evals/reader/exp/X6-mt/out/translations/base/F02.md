# Notice: Cafeteria Closure and Alternative Lunchbox Application (11/16–11/20)

Hello. This is the Welfare Team.
Due to the replacement of the cafeteria's drainage pipes, cafeteria operations will be suspended for the period below. All production and office staff who come to work during this period may apply for alternative lunchboxes. Please review the details below.

**1. Cafeteria Closure and Reopening**
- Suspension period: Mon 11/16 – Fri 11/20
- Normal operations resume: Mon 11/23, 11:30

**2. Alternative Lunchbox Application Details**
- Eligibility: All production and office staff who come to work during the suspension period (applying is optional; only those who need it should apply)
- How to apply: Select the date(s) you will use the service in the in-house app "Bapmaru"
- Application deadline: **Thu 11/12, 16:00** (applications are required for each date of use)
- Quantity: 1 meal per employee per workday (lunchboxes cannot be ordered for days off)
- Menu: Choose one of Regular or Vegetarian (both types cannot be ordered at the same time)
- Employee contribution: 2,000 KRW per meal, regardless of type

**3. Lunchbox Pickup**
- Location: Temporary serving station on the 1st floor of the factory
- Day shift workers: On the day you applied, 11:30 – 12:30
- Night shift workers: On the day you applied, 20:00 – 21:00

**4. If Food Allergies Prevent Use of Both Menus**
- Report: Notify the Welfare Team by **Thu 11/12, 16:00**. You can then receive meal expense support without applying for lunchboxes.
- Support amount: 5,000 KRW for each actual workday during the period
- Proof: Purchase receipts must be submitted to Bapmaru by **Mon 11/23, 12:00**

**5. Cancellation Policy**
- Applications can be cancelled in Bapmaru until Fri 11/13, 12:00.
- After that, cancellations and refunds of the employee contribution are not possible.

**6. Contact**
- Welfare Team: Ko Ha-rin (Ext. 462)
- For questions about lunchbox pickup and allergy reporting

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation. Thank you.

**Somraesit Food Welfare Team**
