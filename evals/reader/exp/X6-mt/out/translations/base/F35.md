**Subject: [Notice] Changes to Submission and Review Rules for Online Live Course Materials (Effective for Courses Starting 12/14 and Later)**

Dear instructors and course operations managers at Daonsel Education Online,

Because course materials have been updated late, some students have received outdated files. To prevent this, we have moved up the material submission deadline and revised the review process. Please review the details below.

**1. Scope and Effective Date**
- Target: Instructors and operations managers of online live courses
- Pre-recorded courses will continue to follow the existing material registration rules.
- Effective: Courses starting Monday, 12/14
- Key change: The material submission deadline is moved from 1 day before the course start to **3 days before the course start**.

**2. Instructor Guidelines**
- Upload the final PDF to the course-specific "Distributed Materials" section in the "Baeumgyeol" system by 18:00, 3 days before the course start.
- Submit one PDF per course, no larger than 20MB.
- If you have multiple documents, please combine them into a single PDF before uploading.

**3. Operations Manager Guidelines**
- By 12:00, 2 days before the course start, confirm that the file opens correctly and that the course name matches, then mark the review as complete in the system.

**4. Material Changes After the Submission Deadline**
- After the deadline, instructors cannot replace files directly.
- To correct content errors, submit a request through the "Correction Request" menu. Once the operations manager approves it, the manager will replace the file.
- If you find a content error during class, you may correct it verbally right away. Even in this case, you must submit a "Correction Request" within 2 hours after the class ends.

**5. Reopening the Same Course**
- If you reopen the same course with no changes to its materials, you may reuse the existing file.
- In this case, click the "Use Existing Materials" button before the submission deadline. No re-upload is necessary.

**6. Rules Acknowledgment Request**
- Both instructors and operations managers must click the rules acknowledgment button on the "Baeumgyeol" notice board by 17:00 on Thursday, 12/10.

For reference, the material submission deadline for courses starting Monday, 12/14 is 18:00 on Friday, 12/11, and the manager review deadline is 12:00 on Saturday, 12/12. Because these dates fall over a weekend, you may want to include the specific dates in the notice.
