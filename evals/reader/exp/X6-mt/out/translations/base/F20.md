## 3. Response Procedure

### 3-1. Initiating Alarm Response
- When relative humidity of **60% or higher is displayed for 10 consecutive minutes** on the monitoring screen **Bo-nun (보존눈)**, begin alarm response.
- On-duty staff must arrive on site **within 5 minutes** after response begins. (Mandatory)

### 3-2. On-Site Measurement and Judgment
Measure using a handheld meter.

| Handheld meter reading | Action |
|---|---|
| **58% or higher** | Operate portable dehumidifier (Mandatory) |
| **Below 58%** | No operation required. Request Ryu Dohyeon to inspect the monitoring sensor |

### 3-3. Portable Dehumidifier Usage Rules
- Use **only** the dedicated outlets in B Preservation Room.
- Operate **no more than 2 units** simultaneously.
- **Do not use** hallway outlets or extension cords.

### 3-4. Re-measurement 20 Minutes After Operation
Measure again with the handheld meter.

| Re-measurement reading | Action |
|---|---|
| **58% or higher** | Request Cha Subin to decide on document relocation |
| **Below 58%** | Dehumidifier may be stopped. It may continue running, but **must be stopped before the end of the duty shift** |

> **Note:** On-duty staff must not relocate documents alone. Cha Subin decides on document relocation.

## 4. Records
- Items to record: **Alarm occurrence time, on-site measurement readings, actions taken**
- Recording location: Bo-nun
- Recording deadline: **Within 40 minutes** after alarm response begins
- Records **must** be kept even when the dehumidifier was not operated (below 58%).
- Shifts without an alarm do not require a separate alarm response record.

## 5. Equipment Return
- Im Nagyeom returns the temporary equipment by **12/17 (Thu) 20:30**.
