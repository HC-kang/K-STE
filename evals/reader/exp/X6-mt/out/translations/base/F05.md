**Subject: [Company-Wide Notice] November Shared Equipment Asset Inventory (Confirmation in 'Byeoljangbu' Required by 11/24 at 17:00)**

To all research and administrative staff of Byeolgori Research Institute,

We will conduct a shared equipment asset inventory in November to resolve discrepancies between the equipment management ledger and the actual storage locations. All employees must review the details below and participate by the deadlines.

## 1. Inventory Scope
- **Covered:** Laptops, tablets, and measuring instruments with company asset numbers
- **Excluded:** Personally owned equipment and consumables without asset numbers

## 2. Required Action for All Employees (Deadline: Tue., 11/24, 17:00)
Check the list of equipment assigned to you in the virtual asset system **'Byeoljangbu'** and mark it as **'Confirmed.'**
- **If no equipment is assigned to you**, you must still mark **'No Assignment'** by the same deadline. No separate email report is required.

## 3. Information Required from Equipment Holders
For each assigned piece of equipment, enter the following in Byeoljangbu:
- Asset number
- Current location
- One photo attachment showing the asset number

## 4. Handling by Equipment Condition

| Condition | Procedure |
|---|---|
| **Operating normally** | Online confirmation in Byeoljangbu is sufficient. No need to bring it to the inventory room. |
| **Malfunctioning** | In addition to online confirmation, return the equipment to the **Inventory Room on the 1st floor of the Research Building** on Wed., 11/25, 10:00–16:00. |
| **Sent to an external repair vendor** | Direct return is exempt. Attach the **repair receipt** in Byeoljangbu by Tue., 11/24, 17:00. The receipt may substitute for the asset number photo. |

## 5. Additional Verification by Team Leads
If an employee has **3 or more** assigned devices, the team lead must provide additional verification. Team leads with such team members must approve in Byeoljangbu by Thu., 11/26, 12:00.

## 6. Reporting Unregistered Equipment
If you discover equipment during the inventory that is not in the management ledger, you must **report it to the Asset Team regardless of whether it has an asset number**. Employees must not dispose of it or transfer it to others on their own.

## 7. Online Entry Briefing (Optional)
- **Date/Time:** Fri., 11/20, 15:00–15:30
- **Location:** Internal video conference room "Byeolgyosil"
- Attendance is optional. **Non-attendance does not change the inventory obligation.**
