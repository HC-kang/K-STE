## 2. Background of the Trial
Due to an increase in reports of corner damage on products using the existing cushioning material, a trial will be conducted to verify the effectiveness of the new cushioning material.

## 3. Trial Scope
| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Date/Time | Thursday, November 5, 09:00–12:00 |
| Location | Factory 1, Packaging Line 2 |
| Target | 240 boxes with the new cushioning material |
| Excluded | Packaging Line 1 (no receipt of new cushioning material or registration of trial results required) |

## 4. Required Actions by Responsible Persons
**Yoon Se-ram (Production Technology)**
- Place 300 units of the new cushioning material at the Packaging Line 2 material station **by Wednesday, November 4, 16:00**

**Do Ha-jun (Packaging Line 2 Team Leader)**
- Inspect the corner condition of **all** 240 trial boxes
- Register the results in the "Packaging Results" system **by Thursday, November 5, 15:00**

## 5. Decision Criteria During the Trial
| Number of Corner Damages Found | Action |
|---|---|
| **5 boxes or more** | **Immediately stop** the trial and Do Ha-jun **reports by phone** to Yoon Se-ram |
| **4 boxes or fewer** | Continue the scheduled 240-box trial |

※ Any additional trial beyond 240 boxes is permitted **only with approval from Yoon Se-ram**.
