**Subject: [Nurigyeol Temperature-Controlled Logistics] Delivery Time Change Notice for Eastern Refrigerated Center Shipments (11/12–11/14) and Request for Pickup Availability Submission**

Hello, store owner.
This is the Delivery Operations Team at Nurigyeol Temperature-Controlled Logistics.

Due to floor repair work at the Eastern Refrigerated Center, delivery times for shipments dispatched during the period below will be temporarily changed. We apologize for the inconvenience.

**1. Delivery Time Change Notice**
- Target: Food stores shipped from the Eastern Refrigerated Center
- Change period: Shipments dispatched 11/12 (Thu) – 11/14 (Sat)
- Delivery time: Previously 07:00–09:00 → **10:00–12:00**
- Starting with shipments dispatched 11/16 (Mon), the original delivery time (07:00–09:00) will resume.

**2. Required Actions for Store Owners (Mandatory)**
Store owners in the target group must submit whether they can receive deliveries between 10:00–12:00 via the "Delivery Change Confirmation" section of the customer portal by **11/10 (Tue) 18:00**.

- If your store cannot receive deliveries between 10:00–12:00, you must select **pickup at a nearby partner cold storage facility** on the same submission screen.
- Stores that select a cold storage facility should visit the facility to pick up their orders on the delivery day between **13:00–17:00**. The storage fee will be covered by our company.

**3. Western Refrigerated Center Shipments**
Shipments from the Western Refrigerated Center will keep their original delivery times, and no delivery change confirmation submission is required.

**4. Delivery Conditions**
- The maximum delivery weight per order remains **40 kg** during the change period.
- There are no additional delivery fees.

**5. Inquiries**
- For questions about selecting a pickup location: Contact Delivery Operations Team representative Ko Se-rin or use "Delivery Inquiry" in the customer portal.
- To change order quantities: This can be done on the existing order placement screen.

Thank you for your continued trust in us.

Sincerely,
Delivery Operations Team, Nurigyeol Temperature-Controlled Logistics
