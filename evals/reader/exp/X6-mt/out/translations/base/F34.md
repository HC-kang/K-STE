**Subject: [Notice] Changes to Walkie-Talkie Rental and Return Policy (Effective from Monday, 12/07 shifts)**

**To:** Venue guide staff and on-site supervisors, Ormit Performance venue
**From:** Operations Team
**Effective:** Shifts starting Monday, 12/07

---

Hello.

Due to low walkie-talkie battery levels and missed returns that have delayed guidance for upcoming performances, we are changing the walkie-talkie rental and return policy as follows. Please review the details carefully and comply starting on the effective date.

## 1. Scope
- Venue guide staff and on-site supervisors
- Staff from external rental companies use their own equipment and are excluded from this rental policy.

## 2. Key Changes
- **Verbal rental requests are discontinued.**
- All walkie-talkie rentals and returns must be **recorded in the "Mudae-On" system.**

## 3. Requirements for Guide Staff

**Rental**
- Collect your equipment from the equipment room on the 1st floor **at least 20 minutes before your shift starts**, using **your own employee ID badge**. **Proxy collection is prohibited.**
- The default allocation is **one unit per person.**
- Upon collection, always confirm that the battery is **at least 80%**.
  - If it is below 80%, request a replacement from the equipment manager and enter the **reason for replacement** in the rental record.

**Return**
- Return the unit to the charging station in the 1st-floor equipment room **within 15 minutes after your shift ends**, and mark the return as **completed** in "Mudae-On."

**Consecutive Shifts**
- If the gap between two shifts is **30 minutes or less**, the on-site supervisor may approve continued use in the system, and you may keep the unit without returning it in between.
- In this case, return the unit **within 15 minutes after the last shift ends.**

## 4. Requirements for On-Site Supervisors
- You may rent **2 additional spare walkie-talkies** per performance.
- Approve continued-use requests from staff on consecutive shifts in "Mudae-On."
- **Within 40 minutes after the last performance of the day ends**, check the list of unreturned units and send it to the equipment manager, **Ha Nu-gyeom.**

## 5. Required Advance Action
- All affected staff must confirm their **employee ID badge number** in "Mudae-On" **by 16:00 on Friday, 12/04.**

## 6. Contact
- Equipment manager Ha Nu-gyeom, extension **573**

Thank you.
