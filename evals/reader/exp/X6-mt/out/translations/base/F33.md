**Subject: [Policy Change Notice] Revised Customer Visit Transportation Expense Settlement Standards (Effective 12/01)**

To Field Designers and Project Managers at Seornendesign,

We have seen a growing number of cases where transportation expense claims for customer visits are rejected because the project code was missing. To reduce these cases, the policy has been revised as follows. Please review the details below.

## 1. Scope
- **Customer visit transportation expenses** for field designers and project managers
- Commuting expenses are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Effective Date
- Applies to **usage from 12/01 (Tue) onward**.

## 3. Key Changes

**① Revised Taxi Fare Limit**
- Per-use taxi fare limit for customer visits: 35,000 KRW → **28,000 KRW**

**② Registration and Approval Process**

| Role | Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| User | Register receipt and project code in the "Customer Visit" menu of the 'Bieyong-it' system (required) | 18:00 on the next business day after use |
| Project Manager | Approve registered items | 15:00 on the next business day after registration |
| Manager's own usage | Approved by Operations Team Leader Do Yeo-ul | 15:00 on the next business day after registration |

**③ Equipment Transport Exception**
- Visits requiring equipment transport at the customer's request are eligible for up to **45,000 KRW** per trip.
- System approval from Operations Team Leader Do Yeo-ul must be obtained before boarding the taxi.
- Be sure to attach the customer's email requesting the transport.
- Amounts exceeding the limit without prior approval are the **employee's personal responsibility**.

**④ Claim Guidelines**
- Splitting a single receipt into multiple claims is prohibited.
- Passengers do not need to submit separate claims; only the person who actually paid may submit the claim.

## 4. Transitional Provisions
- Transportation expenses incurred through 11/30 (Mon) are subject to the **previous limit**.
- These items must be registered by **18:00 on 12/03 (Thu)**, and do not need to be adjusted to the new limit.

## 5. Advance Preparation
- Employees subject to this policy must set a **default project code** in their 'Bieyong-it' profile by **17:00 on 11/27 (Fri)**.
