**[Incident Notice] Automatic Measurement Data Save Interruption on Line 3, "Gyeolcheck," Factory No. 1 (Tue. 12/08)**

We are sharing this with the quality inspectors and production team leaders of Factory No. 1 at Onsaemi Manufacturing.

**■ Situation**
- Starting at 08:30 on Tue. 12/08, automatic saving of measurement data from the "Gyeolcheck" Line 3 has been interrupted.
- Automatic saving on Lines 1 and 2 is operating normally.
- Cause: Insufficient storage space on the Line 3 transmission gateway. The displayed values on the measurement equipment itself are normal.
- Recovery owner: Yun Garam, Equipment Team
- Recovery target: Tue. 12/08, 10:30

**■ Line 3 Production Team Leaders**
1. **Immediately stop loading new production** on Line 3. (Lines 1 and 2 may continue production.)
2. Report the held quantity to the #1 Factory-Quality channel **by 09:20 on Tue. 12/08**. Quantities should be reported in **pieces (ea)**, not boxes.

**■ Line 3 Inspectors**
1. Products manufactured after 08:30 must undergo **full manual inspection**.
2. Enter the product number, measured values, and inspection time on **Record Sheet Q-12**. (Maximum of 30 products per record sheet.)
3. Submit the Q-12 record sheets to Team Leader Baek Jihu of the Quality Lab **by 10:00 on Tue. 12/08**.

**■ Classification of Products Requiring Re-inspection**
- Products for which an automatic save completion indicator was confirmed before 08:30: **No re-inspection required**
- All other Line 3 products: **Subject to manual inspection**

**■ Management of Held Products**
- Store products that have completed manual inspection on the **yellow hold racks in Zone B of Factory No. 1**.
- Products must **not be moved or shipped** until shipment approval is granted by Team Leader Baek Jihu of Quality.

**■ Restart Guidance**
- Quality Team Leader Baek Jihu will announce restart approval in the #1 Factory-Quality channel.
- If no restart approval announcement is made after the recovery target time (10:30), continue to maintain the halt on new loading for Line 3.
- Restart approval does not substitute for shipment approval of held products.
