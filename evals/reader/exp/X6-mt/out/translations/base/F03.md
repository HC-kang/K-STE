**Subject: [Notice] Early Closing and Settlement of December Business Expenses Due to Year-End Accounting System Migration**

To all employees of Paodip Design headquarters and branch offices,

We will close December business expenses early due to the year-end accounting system migration. Please complete your settlements according to the schedule and procedures below. Personal expenses are excluded from settlement.

**1. Submission of General Business Expenses (Payments made 12/01 (Tue) to 12/18 (Fri))**
- Scope: Business expenses paid from 12/01 (Tue) to 12/18 (Fri)
- Submitter: The employee who incurred the expense
- Submission deadline: **12/21 (Mon) by 15:00**
- Submission location: Sembagoni > Expense Settlement > December Early Closing (same for headquarters and branches)

**2. Required Items at Submission**
- For each submission, be sure to enter the receipt and the business purpose.
- Paper receipts may be submitted as photo attachments; the originals do not need to be submitted separately.
- For **each expense of 300,000 KRW or more** (including exactly 300,000 KRW), you must attach a **record of prior approval from your team leader** to the general submission materials.

**3. Business Expenses Scheduled for Payment 12/19 (Sat) to 12/31 (Thu) (Exception Expenses)**
- Advance registration: Please register in the "Exception Expenses" menu of Sembagoni **by 12/18 (Fri) 12:00**. Advance registration is mandatory.
- Submission of supporting documents: For expenses registered in advance, please submit the receipt, business purpose, and, where applicable, the record of prior team leader approval **by 12/31 (Thu) 17:00** after the actual payment.

**4. If There Are No Business Expenses in December**
- Reporting a zero amount and submitting a separate certification are not required.

**5. Team Leader Review Deadlines**
- General expenses: Approve or reject **by 12/22 (Tue) 12:00**
- Exception expenses: Approve or reject **by 12/31 (Thu) 18:00**

**6. Payment Schedule**
- General expenses approved by 12/22 (Tue) 12:00 will be paid on **12/24 (Thu)**.
- Exception expenses are excluded from the 12/24 (Thu) payment.

**7. Contacts**
- Supporting documents and amount criteria: Finance Team, Moon Se-on (ext. 731)
- Sembagoni access issues: IT Team, Han Ieru (ext. 284)

Submissions after the deadlines may be difficult, so please submit according to the schedule. Thank you for your cooperation.

Paodip Design Finance Team
