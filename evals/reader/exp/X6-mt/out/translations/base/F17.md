# Cold Raw Material Receiving Hold Runbook (Bundle 4)

**Seori Food East Distribution Center | Additional Inspection of Designated Vehicles Due to Missing Thermometer Calibration**

## 1. Purpose
Because some vehicles were found to have missing thermometer calibration, this procedure requires additional measurement of the core temperature of chilled raw materials on designated vehicles, and hold processing if they fall outside the standard.

## 2. Scope
| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Period | Mon 11/16 ~ Wed 11/18 |
| Location | Dock 2, East Distribution Center |
| Target vehicles | Vehicles with license plate last digits **62 and 84** |
| Non-target vehicles | Existing inspection continues. No additional 3-box measurement or additional hold registration required |

## 3. Roles
| Role | Name | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Receiving inspector | Han Jio | Temperature measurement, recording, hold decision and registration |
| Quality duty officer | Jung Dabin | Decides whether held materials are usable (approve/return) |
| Warehouse supervisor | Yoo Taegeon | Assigns storage location in Zone Q |

## 4. Time Standards (based on vehicle arrival time)
| Timing | Required Action |
|---|---|
| Within 15 minutes of arrival | Complete core temperature measurement of 3 raw material boxes |
| Within 30 minutes of arrival | If subject to hold, complete hold registration in the cold storage system |
| Within 60 minutes of hold registration | Jung Dabin records approval or return decision in the cold storage system |

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Target Verification
1. Confirm that the license plate last digits of the vehicle arriving at Dock 2 are 62 or 84.
2. If not, proceed with existing inspection only and end this runbook.
3. If so, record the arrival time and proceed to 5.2.

### 5.2 Temperature Measurement (Han Jio)
1. Within **15 minutes** of arrival, measure the core temperature of **3** raw material boxes.
2. Record **all three** measurement values.

### 5.3 Judgment
| Condition | Judgment |
|---|---|
| All three values between 0.0℃ and 5.0℃ inclusive, and no box seal damage | **Normal receiving** |
| Any one value below 0.0℃ or above 5.0℃ | **Full hold** of raw materials from that vehicle |
| Temperature within range but box seal damage found | **Full hold** of raw materials from that vehicle |

- A hold due to seal damage **cannot be released by re-measurement alone.**

### 5.4 Hold Processing (when hold is judged)
1. Move the held raw materials to **Refrigerator Zone Q**. Yoo Taegeon assigns the storage location.
2. Attach a **yellow hold tag** to the held raw materials. (Required)
3. Within **30 minutes** of arrival, register the hold in the inventory system **"Cold Storage" (찬고)**. Attaching site photos is optional.
4. **Production release is prohibited** until Jung Dabin approves.

### 5.5 Usability Decision (Jung Dabin)
1. Within **60 minutes** of hold registration, record an **approve or return** decision in the cold storage system.
2. Held materials remain in Zone Q until an approval is recorded.

## 6. Checklist (per target vehicle)
- [ ] Confirm license plate last digits 62/84, record arrival time
- [ ] Within 15 minutes, measure core temperature of 3 boxes, record all three values
- [ ] Confirm all three values are within 0.0–5.0℃
- [ ] Check for box seal damage
- [ ] (If held) Move to Zone Q and attach yellow hold tag
- [ ] (If held) Register in cold storage system within 30 minutes
- [ ] (If held) Confirm Jung Dabin's approve/return record (within 60 minutes of registration)
- [ ] Confirm no production release before approval
