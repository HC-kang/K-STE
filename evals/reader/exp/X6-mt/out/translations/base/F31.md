**Subject: [Notice] Changes to the Night Emergency Dispatch Approval Process (Effective Mon 11/16, 20:00)**

To all night dispatch staff and dispatch team leaders at Nubeon Logistics,

Because approval records have repeatedly been missing for night emergency dispatches, we are changing the approval process as described below, effective Monday, November 16 at 20:00. The day dispatch team will continue to follow the existing rules.

**1. Scope and Effective Time**
- Applies to: night dispatch staff and dispatch team leaders
- Effective: Monday, November 16, from 20:00. The changes apply to emergency dispatch requests received after the effective time.

**2. Key Changes**
- Phone approvals are discontinued.
- All emergency dispatch requests must be approved by the team leader through the "Emergency Dispatch" menu in the "Jimgyeol" system.

**3. Processing Deadlines**
- Staff: Submit the approval request at least 40 minutes before the scheduled vehicle departure time.
- Team leaders: Complete the approval at least 15 minutes before the scheduled vehicle departure time.

**4. Request Units**
- Each request may include up to 30 boxes.
- If a dispatch exceeds 30 boxes, split it into groups of 30 or fewer and submit a separate approval request for each group.

**5. System Failures**
- Paper emergency dispatch forms may be used only during a system failure.
- The team leader's signature must be obtained before dispatch.
- Within 2 hours after the system is restored, the staff member must enter the record into the system.

**6. Vehicle Number Changes**
- If only the vehicle number changes for an already approved request, the staff member may edit the number directly. A new approval request is not required.

**7. Mandatory Prior Training**
- Target: all staff and team leaders
- Content: Watch the 12-minute video in the "Jimgyeol" training menu and click the confirmation button.
- Deadline: Friday, November 13, 18:00

**8. Inquiries and Failure Reports**
- Operations Support: Heo Daon
- Company messenger channel: "Night Dispatch Inquiries" (Please use the same channel to report system failures.)

Thank you.
