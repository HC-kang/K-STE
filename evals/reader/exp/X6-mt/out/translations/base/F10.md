**[Incident Notice] Visitor QR Issuance Suspended for Visitor Management System "Munpass" and Response Guidelines**

Hello. We are sharing details of the incident and response procedures with headquarters visit-scheduling staff and first-floor reception desk personnel.

**1. Incident Status**
- Start time: 12/17 (Thu), from 08:50
- Details: Issuance of visitor QR codes in the visitor management system "Munpass" has been suspended
- Cause: Expired key for the text message sending integration
- Recovery owner: Somang Lee, Facilities IT Team
- Recovery target: 12/17 (Thu), 13:00 (Once recovery is complete, an announcement will be posted in the #HQ-Visitor-Guide channel)

**2. Scope of Impact**
- Employee access cards are operating normally.
- Visitor QR codes issued before the incident remain usable within their validity period, and no paper visitor pass is required in that case.

**3. Guidance for Visit-Scheduling Staff**

■ Visitors scheduled between 09:00 and 12:00 on 12/17 (Thu) who do not have a valid QR code
- Submission of a temporary visitor list is mandatory.
- Required entries: visitor name, visit time, host employee's extension number
- Submission form: internal form "1217 Visit Alternative"
- Deadline: 09:20 on 12/17 (Thu)

■ Visit reservations added after 09:20 for times before 12:00
- Instead of submitting the form, you must register the visit by phone with the reception desk (extension 107).
- Please register at least 20 minutes before the scheduled visit time.

■ Visit reservations after 12:00
- No temporary visitor list submission is required at this time.
- Please proceed after checking the follow-up announcement from the Operations Team, posted at 11:30 on 12/17 (Thu).

■ Escorting visitors who use paper passes
- Visitors receiving paper passes must be met and escorted by their host employee directly at the first floor.
- Visitors using a valid QR code do not require any additional escort due to this incident.

**4. Paper Pass Operations (Reception Desk)**
- Visitors without a valid QR code may receive a paper pass only at the first-floor reception desk of headquarters. Issuance is not available at the 3rd-floor reception room.
- One pass is issued per visitor, and it is valid until 18:00 on the same day.
- Passes must be returned to the reception desk upon departure.
- Reception desk staff must report the number of paper passes issued and returned to Haeon Choi, General Affairs Team, by 18:20 on 12/17 (Thu).

**5. Contacts**
- Visitor phone registration and pass inquiries: Reception desk, extension 107
- QR system error inquiries: Somang Lee, extension 733

We apologize for the inconvenience to your work. Thank you for your cooperation.
