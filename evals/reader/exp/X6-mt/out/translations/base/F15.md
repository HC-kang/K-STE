# Guest Room Temperature Sensor Replacement Meeting Minutes

**Blue Knot Hotel | Meeting to Confirm the Guest Room Temperature Sensor Replacement Sequence**

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Date & Time | Monday, December 14, 2026, 09:00–09:30 |
| Location | Hotel basement operations meeting room |
| Agenda | Confirm the replacement sequence for guest room temperature sensors |
| Recipients | Guest room supervisor, facilities on-duty staff |

## 1. Background
Repeated discrepancies were found between the displayed temperature and the actual measured temperature in rooms on the 7th floor, so it was decided to replace the sensors.

## 2. Scope of Work
- **Target:** 7th floor, Rooms 701–712, 12 rooms in total
- **Excluded:** Rooms on the 8th floor are excluded from this work, and no access preparation is required.

## 3. Responsibilities

| Responsible Party | Task | Deadline / Time |
|---|---|---|
| Facilities manager Lee Doha | Replace sensors in target rooms (one new sensor per room) | Wed 12/16, 10:00–14:00 |
| Guest room supervisor Jo Haerin | Register occupancy/checkout status of target rooms in the "Ruimeum" work schedule (required) | By Tue 12/15, 18:00 |
| Guest room supervisor Jo Haerin | Mark rooms without guest entry consent as "separate schedule required" (required) | By Wed 12/16, 15:00 |
| Facilities on-duty staff Im Seowoo | Verify replaced rooms for 20 minutes using a portable meter (required) | After replacement is complete |
| Guest room supervisor Jo Haerin | Resume sales for rooms that pass verification | From Wed 12/16, 16:00 |

## 4. Work Sequence and Principles
1. Work begins with **vacant rooms** as of Wed 12/16, 10:00.
2. **Occupied rooms** may only be worked on if the guest has consented to entry.
3. Rooms without guest entry consent are excluded from the day's work. Jo Haerin marks them as "separate schedule required" by Wed 12/16, 15:00.

## 5. Post-Replacement Verification and Resumption of Sales
- Im Seowoo verifies each replaced room for **20 minutes** using a portable meter. **Sales cannot be resumed before verification.**
- The pass criterion is the difference between the displayed value and the measured value.

| Difference | Action |
|---|---|
| 1.5°C or less | Im Seowoo records as **pass** |
| More than 1.5°C | **Keep the sales hold**, and Lee Doha **re-inspects** (required) |

- Rooms that pass verification may resume sales from Wed 12/16, 16:00. Resuming sales on the same day is **optional**.
