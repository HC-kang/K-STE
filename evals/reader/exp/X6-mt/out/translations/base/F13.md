# Meeting Minutes: Confirmation of Returned Equipment Receiving Procedure

**Daonbit Rental, West Logistics Center**

| Item | Details |
|---|---|

| Location | West Logistics Center Training Room |
| Agenda | Confirmation of returned equipment receiving procedure |
| Attendees | Collection drivers, receiving staff |

## 1. Background of Change
Traffic lines for returned equipment and outbound equipment overlapped, which delayed loading. To resolve this, the returned equipment receiving procedure will be changed as follows.

## 2. Scope of Application
- **Start date:** Wednesday, November 18
- **Target:** Mobile air coolers returned to the West Logistics Center
- **Excluded:** Returns to the East Logistics Center

## 3. Confirmed Procedure

### 3-1. Advance Registration (Collection Drivers)
- Vehicle numbers and return quantities must be registered in the '돌아온' system by 16:00 the day before the visit.

### 3-2. Scheduled Return Criteria
| Category | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Return time | 10:00~12:00 |
| Quantity per vehicle | Up to 8 air coolers |
| Default unloading location | Dock No. 3, West Logistics Center |

- If the return quantity exceeds 8 units, collection drivers must split the load across vehicles and **book each vehicle separately**.
- Excess units may not be loaded onto a single vehicle for return.

### 3-3. Equipment with Water Leak Traces
- Collection drivers must contact receiving staff member **Seo Na-gyeom** before unloading.
- Such equipment must be unloaded at **Quarantine Zone Q**, not at Dock No. 3.

### 3-4. Same-Day Emergency Returns
- Returns without prior system registration are permitted only with advance approval from dispatch manager **Ko Si-on**.
- The per-vehicle quantity limit (maximum 8 units) applies equally.

### 3-5. Post-Receiving Photo Registration (Receiving Staff)
- Receiving staff member **Seo Na-gyeom** must take two exterior photos of each unit and attach them to the system.
- This must be completed **by 17:00 on the day of return**.
