**Subject: [Notice] Reduction of Analysis Sample Storage Period and Changes to Management Procedures (Effective 11/23)**

**To:** Analysis Lab Researchers and Sample Management Staff, Morafit Research
**From:** (Sending department/person in charge)
**Effective date:** Monday, November 23

---

**1. Background of Change**

Samples past their storage deadline continue to occupy freezer space. To address this, the storage period and the procedures for handling expired samples are changed as follows.

**2. Scope**

- Analysis lab researchers and sample management staff
- Educational model samples are excluded from this policy.

**3. Key Changes**

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Default storage period | 60 days → **30 days** |
| Application | Applies to **actual analysis samples newly received from Monday, 11/23** |
| Existing samples | Samples received before Monday, 11/23 keep the existing 60-day storage period. No re-registration under the new policy is required. |

**4. Procedures and Deadlines by Task**

| Task | Responsible | Deadline | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Register new samples | Receiving researcher | By 17:00 on the day of receipt | "Sample Tteul" > "Receipt Registration" |
| Print and attach labels | Sample management staff | By 12:00 on the next business day after registration | Print the label from the system and attach it to the sample container |
| Request storage extension | Receiving researcher | By 17:00, 3 days before the expiration date | Submit via "Sample Tteul" (requires approval from Lab Director Yeon Bo-min) |
| Move expired samples | Sample management staff | By 16:00 on the next business day after expiration | Move to the 2nd-floor disposal waiting area |

- Sample management staff will move expired samples. Researchers do not need to move them personally.

**5. Storage Extension Criteria**

- Extension is allowed once per sample, for a maximum of 15 days.
- Extending a storage period without approval from the Lab Director is prohibited.

**6. Required Advance Confirmation**

Researchers and sample management staff must complete the confirmation check for the revised policy in "Sample Tteul" by **17:00 on 11/20 (Friday)**.

**7. Contact**

Research Support: Bae So-un, ext. 462
