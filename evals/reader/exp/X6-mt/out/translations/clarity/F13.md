# Meeting Minutes: Return Equipment Receiving Procedure

**Audience:** Recovery drivers and receiving staff at the Daonbit Rental Western Logistics Center

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Meeting date and time | Monday, November 16, 2026, 08:30–09:00 |
| Location | Training Room, Western Logistics Center |
| Agenda | Finalize the procedure for receiving returned equipment |

## 1. Background of the Change
Travel routes for returned equipment and outbound equipment overlapped, causing delays in loading. To resolve this, the return receiving procedure will be changed as follows.

## 2. Scope and Start Date
- **Start date:** Wednesday, November 18, 2026
- **Scope:** Mobile cooling fans returned to the Western Logistics Center
- **Excluded:** Returns to the Eastern Logistics Center

## 3. Finalized Procedure

### 3-1. Advance Registration (Recovery Drivers)
- Vehicle numbers and return quantities must be registered in the "Dolawa" system by 16:00 the day before the visit.

### 3-2. Scheduled Return Criteria
| Category | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Return time | 10:00–12:00 |
| Maximum quantity per vehicle | 8 cooling fans |
| Default unloading location | Dock No. 3, Western Logistics Center |

- If the return quantity exceeds 8 units, the recovery driver must split the load across vehicles and make a **separate reservation for each vehicle**.
- Loading the excess units onto a single vehicle for return is not permitted.

### 3-3. Equipment with Signs of Water Leakage
- Recovery drivers must contact receiving staff member **Seo Na-gyeom** before unloading.
- Such equipment must be unloaded at **Quarantine Zone Q**, not at Dock No. 3.

### 3-4. Same-Day Urgent Returns
- Returns without prior system registration are permitted only with advance approval from dispatch manager **Ko Si-on**.
- The per-vehicle quantity limit (maximum 8 units) still applies.

### 3-5. Post-Receiving Photo Registration (Receiving Staff)
- Receiving staff member **Seo Na-gyeom** must take two exterior photos of each unit and attach them to the system.
- This must be completed by **17:00 on the day of the return**.
