# [Incident Report] Malfunction of the Invoice Printing System (Taekpin) in the Packing Area on the 2nd Floor of the Dongbu Center

To: Dispatch staff and floor supervisors, Nubion Logistics Dongbu Center

**1. Situation**
- Starting at 09:10 on 11/12 (Thu), the invoice printing system "Taekpin" in the 2nd-floor packing area of the Dongbu Center has been malfunctioning.
- The cause is a missed renewal of the invoice server's certificate. The carrier delivery tracking function is operating normally.
- The person responsible for recovery is Seo Do-yun of the IT Operations Team. The recovery target is 11:30 on 11/12 (Thu). Actual recovery status will be announced separately in the #Dongbu-center-incident channel.

**2. Action Guidelines for Dispatch Staff**
- Since the malfunction began, **do not use the Taekpin reprint button.**
- Until the recovery announcement, **do not resubmit the same order.**
- Only **urgent orders** to be loaded on the vehicle departing at 12:00 on 11/12 (Thu) may use handwritten invoices.
- For regular orders, complete packing and store them on the **2nd-floor hold shelf**.
- Invoices that were printed before the malfunction and whose barcodes scan correctly may still be used. These orders do not need to be entered in the shared document.

**3. How to Use Handwritten Invoices**
- Receive handwritten invoices from floor supervisor Kang Ha-rin.
- Each dispatch staff member is initially issued 15 sheets. Additional sheets require approval from Kang Ha-rin.
- If you use handwritten invoices, you must enter the order number, invoice number, and number of sheets used in the shared document "November Manual Dispatch" **by 10:50 on 11/12 (Thu)**.

**4. Requests for Floor Supervisors**
- Please report the number of orders on the hold shelf in the #Dongbu-center-incident channel **by 11:00 on 11/12 (Thu)**.

**5. Key Times**

| Time (11/12 Thu) | Details |

| 11:00 | Deadline for reporting hold shelf order count (supervisors) |
