# Subject: [Required] November Shared Equipment Asset Audit (Confirm in 'Byeoljangbu' by Nov. 24 (Tue) 17:00)

To all research and administrative staff at Byeolgori Research Institute,

We will conduct a shared equipment asset audit in November to resolve discrepancies between the equipment management ledger and the actual storage locations. Since all staff must participate, please review the details below and complete the required steps by the deadline.

**1. Audit Scope**
- Target: Laptops, tablets, and measuring instruments with company asset tags
- Excluded: Personally owned equipment and consumables without asset tags

**2. Required for All Staff**
- Check the list of equipment assigned to you in the virtual asset system 'Byeoljangbu' and mark it **"Confirmed" by Nov. 24 (Tue) 17:00**.
- Even if no equipment is assigned to you, you must mark **"No Assignment"** by the same deadline. You do not need to report this by email separately.

**3. Required for Equipment Holders**
- Enter the **asset number and current location** of each piece of equipment in Byeoljangbu.
- Attach **one photo showing the asset number**.

**4. Handling by Equipment Status**

| Equipment Status | How to Handle |
|---|---|
| Working normally | Online confirmation in Byeoljangbu is sufficient. No need to bring it to the audit room. |
| Malfunctioning | In addition to online confirmation, **return it to the Audit Room on the 1st floor of the Research Building on Nov. 25 (Wed), 10:00–16:00**. |
| Sent to an external repair shop | Direct return is exempt. **Attach the repair receipt to Byeoljangbu by Nov. 24 (Tue) 17:00**. A receipt can substitute for the asset number photo. |

**5. Additional Team Leader Confirmation**
- If an individual is assigned **three or more** pieces of equipment, their team leader must provide additional confirmation.
- Team leaders must **approve in Byeoljangbu by Nov. 26 (Thu) 12:00**.

**6. Discovery of Unregistered Equipment**
- Any unregistered equipment found during the audit must be **reported to the Asset Team**, regardless of whether it has an asset tag.
- Do not dispose of it or transfer it to anyone else on your own.

**7. Online Entry Briefing (Optional)**
- Date/Time: Nov. 20 (Fri), 15:00–15:30
- Location: Internal video conference room "Byeolgyosil"
- Attendance is optional. Audit obligations apply equally to those who do not attend.

**8. Key Schedule Summary**

| Date/Time | Event |
