# [Notice] Changes to Customer Visit Transportation Expense Settlement Policy (Effective 12/01)

**Target:** Field designers and project managers at Sernun Design
**Effective date:** Applies to usage from Tuesday, December 1

## 1. Reason for Change
Expense claims for customer visit transportation are increasingly being rejected for settlement due to missing project codes. To reduce this, we are clarifying the registration and approval procedures and deadlines, and adjusting the taxi fare support limit.

## 2. Scope
- **Applies to:** Transportation expenses for customer visits by field designers and project managers
- **Excluded:** Commuting expenses are not covered by this policy.

## 3. Key Changes

### A. Taxi Fare Support Limit
| Category | Previous | Revised |
|---|---|---|
| Customer visit taxi fare (per use) | 35,000 KRW | **28,000 KRW** |

### B. Registration and Approval Deadlines
| Step | Responsible Party | Method | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Registration | Transportation expense user | Register receipt and project code in the "Customer Visit" menu of "Biyong-it" | 18:00 on the next business day after use |
| Approval | Project manager | Approve in Biyong-it | 15:00 on the next business day after registration |
| Approval (manager's own usage) | Operations Team Leader Do Yeo-ul | Approve in Biyong-it | 15:00 on the next business day after registration |

### C. Equipment Transport Exception
Visits requiring equipment transport at the customer's request may be supported up to **45,000 KRW** per trip. All of the following conditions must be met:
1. Obtain system approval from Operations Team Leader Do Yeo-ul **before** boarding the taxi.
2. Attach the customer's email request for transport.

Any amount exceeding the limit incurred without prior approval will be borne by the individual.

### D. Notes on Claims
- Splitting a single receipt into multiple claims is prohibited.
- Fellow passengers do not need to be claimed separately. Only the person who actually paid may claim.

## 4. Transitional Measures
- Transportation expenses incurred through Monday, November 30 are subject to the **previous limit (35,000 KRW)**.
- These items must be registered **by 18:00 on Thursday, December 3**. There is no need to adjust amounts to the new limit.

## 5. Advance Preparation (Required)
Employees subject to this policy must set a **default project code** in their "Biyong-it" profile **by 17:00 on Friday, November 27**.
