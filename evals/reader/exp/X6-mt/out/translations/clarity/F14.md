## 2. Background to the Change in Operating Method

In the previous training, hands-on practice was delayed by differences in the settings of trainees' personal laptops. To prevent this, starting with this training we will use rented laptops by default and install and check the practice files in advance.

## 4. Decisions

### 4.1 Preparation of Practice Files (Instructor)
- Instructor Ha Yun-yoon must upload the practice files to the instructor materials folder in the Baeumkan system **by 12:00 on Friday, December 4**.

### 4.2 Preparation of Rented Laptops (Operations Staff)
- Operations staff member Mun Tae-gyeom must copy the practice files to all 24 rented laptops and confirm that they run. The deadline is **17:00 on Monday, December 7**.

### 4.3 Criteria for Personal Laptop Use
- In principle, personal laptops may not be used.
- As an exception, trainees who require assistive accessibility software may use a personal laptop only with **prior approval** from operations manager Cha Yeo-jin.
- Approved personal laptops must also be checked to confirm that the practice files run. Operations staff member Mun Tae-gyeom will complete this check by **12:30 on Tuesday, December 8**.

### 4.4 Supplementary Practice and End Time
- Supplementary practice that runs without an external network connection may be added at the instructor's discretion.
- Even if supplementary practice is included, the training end time remains **16:00**.

### 4.5 Textbooks
- Printed textbooks will not be distributed. Neither the instructors nor the operations staff need to print textbooks.
- Trainees will use the Baeumkan e-textbooks.
