## 3. Response Procedure

### 3-1. Start of Alarm Response
- Start the alarm response when the monitoring screen **"Bo-Jun-Nun"** shows relative humidity at **60% or higher for 10 consecutive minutes**.
- On-duty staff must arrive on site **within 5 minutes** of starting the response.

### 3-2. On-Site Measurement and Dehumidifier Decision
Measure humidity with the handheld meter on site.

| Handheld meter reading | Action |
|---|---|
| **58% or higher** | **Must** operate the portable dehumidifier. |
| **Below 58%** | No operation needed. Request **inspection of the monitoring sensor** from Ryu Do-hyeon. |

### 3-3. Portable Dehumidifier Usage Rules
- Use the **dedicated outlet** for B Archive Room.
- A **maximum of 2 units** may operate at the same time.
- Do **not** use hallway outlets or extension cords.

### 3-4. Re-measurement 20 Minutes After Operation
Measure again with the handheld meter 20 minutes after starting the dehumidifier.

| Re-measurement reading | Action |
|---|---|
| **58% or higher** | Request a **document relocation decision** from Cha Su-bin. |
| **Below 58%** | The dehumidifier may be stopped or kept running. (However, it **must be stopped before the end of the on-duty shift**.) |

> **Caution:** On-duty staff **do not relocate documents alone.** Document relocation requires a decision from Cha Su-bin.

### 3-5. Recording
- Record the following in **"Bo-Jun-Nun"** within **40 minutes** of starting the alarm response:
  - Time the alarm occurred
  - On-site measurement readings
  - Actions taken
- Recording is **mandatory** even when the dehumidifier was not operated (for example, when the handheld reading was below 58%).
- Shifts without an alarm do not need a separate alarm response record.
