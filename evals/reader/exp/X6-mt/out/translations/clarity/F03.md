**Subject: [Notice] Early Closing and Settlement of December Business Expenses Due to Year-End Accounting System Migration**

To all employees of Padoip Design headquarters and branch offices,

Due to the year-end migration of our accounting system, we will close December business expense settlements early. Please review the schedule and procedures below and submit your claims by the deadlines. Personal expenses are not eligible for settlement.

**1. Schedule Summary**

| Category | Deadline | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| General expense submission | 12/21 (Mon) 15:00 | Business expenses paid 12/01 (Tue) to 12/18 (Fri) |
| Exception expense pre-registration | 12/18 (Fri) 12:00 | Business expenses scheduled for payment 12/19 (Sat) to 12/31 (Thu) |
| Exception expense receipt submission | 12/31 (Thu) 17:00 | Pre-registered exception expenses |
| Team leader review (general expenses) | 12/22 (Tue) 12:00 | Approve or reject |
| Team leader review (exception expenses) | 12/31 (Thu) 18:00 | Approve or reject |

**2. General Expenses (Payments from 12/01 to 12/18)**
- The employee who incurred the expense must submit it in the Sembagoni system by 12/21 (Mon) 15:00.
- Submission path: Sembagoni > Expense Settlement > December Early Closing (same for headquarters and branch offices)
- For each submission, be sure to enter the receipt and the business purpose.
- Paper receipts may be substituted with photo attachments. You do not need to submit the originals separately.

**3. Expenses of KRW 300,000 or More per Item**
- In addition to the above submission materials, you must attach the team leader's prior approval record.
- Items of exactly KRW 300,000 are also included.

**4. Exception Expenses (Scheduled Payments from 12/19 to 12/31)**
- You must pre-register them by 12/18 (Fri) 12:00 through the "Exception Expenses" menu in Sembagoni.
- After actual payment of pre-registered items, please submit the receipt and business purpose by 12/31 (Thu) 17:00. For items requiring team leader prior approval, such as those of KRW 300,000 or more, the approval record must also be submitted.

**5. Team Leader Review**
- Team leaders are requested to approve or reject general expenses by 12/22 (Tue) 12:00 and exception expenses by 12/31 (Thu) 18:00.

**6. Payment Information**
- General expenses approved by 12/22 (Tue) 12:00 will be paid on 12/24 (Thu).
- Exception expenses are excluded from the 12/24 (Thu) payment.

**7. If You Have No Business Expenses for December**
- You do not need to submit a zero-amount report or a separate confirmation.

**8. Contacts**
- Receipts and amount criteria: Moon Se-on, Finance Team (ext. 731)
- Sembagoni access issues: Han Iru, IT Team (ext. 284)

Thank you.
