# Cold Raw Material Receiving Hold Runbook (Bundle 4)

**Seori-on Foods East Distribution Center | Additional Inspection of Designated Vehicles Due to Missing Thermometer Calibration**

## 1. Purpose

Some vehicles were found to have missing thermometer calibration. Cold raw materials from designated vehicles will undergo additional inspection. Materials that fall outside the standard will be held, and they will not be released to production without approval from the quality duty officer.

## 2. Scope

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Period | 11/16 (Mon) to 11/18 (Wed) |
| Location | Dock 2, East Distribution Center |
| Target vehicles | Vehicles whose license plate numbers end in **62** or **84** |
| Non-target vehicles | Existing inspection continues. No additional 3-box measurement or additional hold registration. |

## 3. Responsible Personnel

| Role | Person | Duties |
|---|---|---|
| Receiving inspector | Han Jio | Temperature measurement and recording, hold registration |
| Quality duty officer | Jung Dabin | Decides whether held materials can be used |
| Warehouse supervisor | Yu Taegeon | Assigns storage locations for held materials |

## 4. Procedure

### 4-1. Measurement (within 15 minutes of vehicle arrival)

1. When a target vehicle arrives, record the arrival time.
2. Select **3 boxes** of raw material and measure the **core temperature** of each.
3. Record **all three** measured values.

### 4-2. Judgment

| Condition | Judgment |
|---|---|
| All three values are between 0.0℃ and 5.0℃ inclusive, and no box seals are damaged | **Normal receiving** |
| Any one value is below 0.0℃ or above 5.0℃ | **Hold all materials** from that vehicle |
| Temperatures are within range, but box seal damage is found | **Hold all materials** from that vehicle |

- Holds apply to the **entire vehicle**, not individual boxes.
- A hold due to seal damage cannot be lifted by re-measurement alone. Release is subject to Jung Dabin's decision.

### 4-3. Actions When Holding

1. Move the held materials to **Refrigerator Zone Q**. Yu Taegeon assigns the storage location.
2. Attach a **yellow hold tag** to the held materials.
3. Register the hold in the inventory system **"Chango"**. The deadline is **within 30 minutes of vehicle arrival**. Attaching on-site photos is optional.
4. Held materials are **prohibited from being released to production** until Jung Dabin approves them.

### 4-4. Quality Duty Officer Decision

- Jung Dabin must record an **approval or return** decision in Chango **within 60 minutes** of the hold registration.

## 5. Time Standards Summary

| Standard | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Core temperature measurement of 3 boxes | Within 15 minutes of vehicle arrival |
| Hold registration in Chango | Within 30 minutes of vehicle arrival |
| Recording approval or return decision | Within 60 minutes of hold registration |
