**Subject: [Notice] Changes to Submission and Review Rules for Online Live Course Materials (Applies to Courses Starting 12/14 and Later)**

To online instructors and course operations managers,

Delays in updating course materials have resulted in outdated files being distributed to students. To prevent this, we have moved up the material submission deadline and added a review process. Please review the details below.

**1. Scope and Effective Date**
- Target: Instructors and operations managers of online live courses
- Effective: Courses starting Monday, 12/14
- Pre-recorded courses continue to follow the existing material registration rules.
- Key change: The material submission deadline is moved up from 1 day before the course start to **3 days before the course start**.

**2. Instructors**
- Upload the final PDF to the course's "Distribution Materials" section in the 'Baeumgyeol' system **by 18:00, 3 days before the course start**.
- Each course requires one PDF file, 20MB or less.
- If you have multiple documents, please combine them into a single PDF before uploading.

**3. Operations Managers**
- **By 12:00, 2 days before the course start**, verify that the file opens and that the course name is correct, then mark the review as complete in the system.

**4. Editing Materials After the Submission Deadline**
- After the submission deadline, instructors cannot replace files directly.
- Submit content errors through the 'Correction Request' menu. Once an operations manager approves the request, the manager will replace the file.
- If you discover a content error during class, instructors may first correct it verbally on the spot. Even in this case, you must submit a 'Correction Request' **within 2 hours after the class ends**.

**5. Reopening the Same Course Without Material Changes**
- You may reuse the existing file, so there is no need to upload it again.
- Instructors must click the "Use Existing Materials" button by the submission deadline (18:00, 3 days before the course start).

**6. Schedule Example (Course Starting Monday, 12/14)**

| Task | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Instructor material upload | Friday, 12/11, 18:00 |
| Operations manager review completion | Saturday, 12/12, 12:00 |

**7. Request to Confirm the Rules**
- Both instructors and operations managers must click the rules confirmation button in the 'Baeumgyeol' notice by **17:00 on Thursday, 12/10**.
