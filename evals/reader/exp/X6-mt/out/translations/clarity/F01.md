**Subject: [Required Reading] Change in Work Account Authentication Method: Register "Gyeoljamgeum" (Nov 9–12)**

Dear employees,

Support for our existing authentication devices has ended, so we are switching work account authentication to the virtual system "Gyeoljamgeum." To continue using your work account after the switch, you must register Gyeoljamgeum within the period below.

**1. Who this applies to**
- All full-time and contract employees
- Excluded: external visitors who do not have a personal work account

**2. Registration period**
- Monday, November 9, 09:00 to Thursday, November 12, 18:00
- Everyone in the target group must register.

**3. How to register**
- Location: Company portal > My Account > Register Gyeoljamgeum
- Please proceed using your own work account.
- Choose **one** of the following registration methods. You do not need to register both.
  - Personal mobile phone app
  - Company security key

**4. Applying for and receiving a security key (if you do not want to use a personal mobile phone)**
- Application: Apply through the company portal by 12:00 on Tuesday, November 10 (one free key per person)
- Pickup: Wednesday, November 11, 13:00 to 17:00, Support Office, 2nd floor, Headquarters
- You **must bring your employee ID badge** when picking up a key.

**5. Employees on leave**
- Employees who are on leave for the entire registration period are exempt from this registration.
- In this case, registration must be completed by 17:00 on the day they return to work.

**6. Effective date of new authentication and notes**
- Effective: Friday, November 13, 09:00
- If you have not completed registration, you will not be able to log in to your work account until you do.
- To avoid work disruption, please register within the registration period.

**7. Contacts**
- Registration errors: Da-on Yu, Information Security Team (company messenger)
- Security key pickup schedule: Soyul Bae, General Affairs Team (company messenger)

Thank you.

Information Security Team, Nurigyeolje Works
