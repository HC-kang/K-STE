**[Company-Wide Notice] Emergency Evacuation Drill on 12/08 (Tue)**

To all employees of Onsaebyeok Logistics,

Due to the change in the emergency exit of the logistics building, an emergency evacuation drill will be held as follows so that everyone can become familiar with the new evacuation route. Delivery drivers, sorting staff, and office staff must all review the details below.

**1. Drill Date and Time**
- 12/08 (Tue), 14:00–14:20 (20 minutes)
- Participants: All delivery drivers, sorting staff, and office staff present at the worksite on that day (participation required)

**2. Drill Procedure**
- When the drill begins, stop your current work and proceed to the changed **east emergency exit**.
- The assembly point is **Outdoor Parking Lot B**.
- **The west main entrance cannot be used during the drill.**
- Parking Lot A, the previous assembly point, will not be used for this drill because equipment is stored there. Please go to Parking Lot B instead.
- After the drill ends, team leaders will confirm headcount at Parking Lot B. **You may not return to work until the headcount is complete.**

**3. Exemptions from On-Site Drill and Alternative Completion**
| Category | On-site drill | Alternative video viewing |
|---|---|---|
| Drivers out on external deliveries at 14:00 | Exempt | **Required** (by 12/09 (Wed) 18:00) |
| Employees on full-day leave on 12/08 (Tue) | Exempt | Exempt |

- How to watch the video: View the evacuation video (8 minutes) on the virtual training app **"Gilbaeum."**
- Completion criteria: You must answer all 4 review questions correctly after watching the video to be marked as complete. If you answer any incorrectly, you may retake the quiz as many times as needed.

**4. Notes for Team Leaders**
- Please submit the list of expected participants to the **Safety Portal** by 16:00 on 12/07 (Mon).
- On the list, please separately mark employees **scheduled for external deliveries** and employees **on full-day leave**.

**5. Employees Requiring Mobility Assistance**
- Please apply to Safety Team member Jung Ra-gyeom (ext. 518) by 15:00 on 12/04 (Fri).
- Requesting an escort is optional.

**6. Precautions**
- If an actual fire or accident occurs during the drill, stop the drill immediately and follow the live emergency-response instructions from the on-site announcements first.

**7. Contacts**
- Questions about the drill or video completion: Safety Team, Jung Ra-gyeom (ext. 518)
- Issues with the participant list submission: Operations Team, Seo Do-yun (ext. 206)

Thank you for your active participation.

Best regards.
