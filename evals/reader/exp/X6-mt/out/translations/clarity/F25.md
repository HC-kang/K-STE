## Background of Change
Repeated brief communication drops cause disconnection alerts to pile up for the same equipment, which is increasing customer support inquiries. To reduce this, we are changing the resend interval.

## Description of Change
When a sensor's disconnection continues, the system will behave as follows:

- The initial disconnection email is sent immediately.
- If the disconnection continues, the alert is resent every 20 minutes.
- The connection restored email is sent immediately, without any wait.

## Scope and Exclusions

| Category | Change applied? |
|---|---|
| Email disconnection alerts for the Standard plan | Applied |
| Enterprise plan | Excluded (existing behavior maintained) |
| SMS alerts | Excluded (existing behavior maintained) |
