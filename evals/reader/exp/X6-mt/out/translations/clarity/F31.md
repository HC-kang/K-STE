**Subject: [Notice] Changes to the Night Emergency Dispatch Approval Process (Effective Mon., Nov. 16, 20:00)**

To night dispatch staff and dispatch team leads at Nubeon Logistics,

Approval records have repeatedly been missing for night emergency dispatches, so we are changing the approval process as described below. Please review the changes and be sure to complete the training before they take effect.

**1. Scope and Effective Time**
- Target: Night dispatch staff and night dispatch team leads. (The daytime dispatch team will continue to follow the existing rules.)
- Effective: Monday, November 16, from 20:00
- Scope: Applies to emergency dispatch requests received at or after the effective time.

**2. Changes**
- Phone approval is discontinued.
- Going forward, approval must be obtained from the team lead through the "Emergency Dispatch" menu in the "Jikgyeol" system.

**3. Deadlines**
- Staff: Submit the approval request at least 40 minutes before the scheduled vehicle departure time.
- Team lead: Complete the approval at least 15 minutes before the scheduled vehicle departure time.

**4. Request Unit**
- Each approval request must cover 30 boxes or fewer.
- Requests exceeding 30 boxes must be split into groups of 30 or fewer, and each group must be approved separately.

**5. System Failure**
- A paper emergency dispatch form may be used only when the system is down.
- The team lead's signature must be obtained before dispatch.
- Within 2 hours of system recovery, the staff member must enter the record into the system.
- Report system failures through the contact channel below.

**6. Vehicle Number Changes**
- If only the vehicle number changes for a request that has already been approved, the staff member may edit the number directly. Re-approval is not required.

**7. Mandatory Training**
- Target: Both staff and team leads
- Content: Watch the 12-minute video in the "Jikgyeol" training menu and click the confirmation button.
- Deadline: Friday, November 13, 18:00

**8. Inquiries**
- Operations Support: Heo Daon
- Company messenger channel "Night Dispatch Inquiries" (Please use this same channel to report system failures.)

Thank you.
