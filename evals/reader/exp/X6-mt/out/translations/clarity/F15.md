## 2. Reason for Replacement

Temperature readings on the 7th floor rooms repeatedly differed from actual measured values, so the sensors will be replaced.

## 3. Work Scope

- **Target:** Rooms 701–712 on the 7th floor, 12 rooms in total
- **Excluded:** Rooms on the 8th floor are not included in this work, and no access preparation is required for them.

## 4. Work Schedule and Assignments

| Date and time | Responsible | Task |
|---|---|---|

| Wednesday, 12/16, 10:00–14:00 | Facilities staff Lee Doha | Replace sensors in target rooms (one new sensor per room) |

## 5. Work Sequence and Criteria

### 5-1. Work Sequence
- Work begins with vacant rooms as of 10:00 on Wednesday, 12/16.
- Occupied rooms may be worked on only if the guest has consented to room entry.
- Rooms without guest consent to entry are excluded from work that day. Supervisor Cho Haerin will mark these rooms "separate schedule required" by 15:00 on Wednesday, 12/16.

### 5-2. Verification After Replacement
Duty staff Im Seowoo will verify each room that has been replaced for 20 minutes with a handheld meter. **Sales cannot resume before verification.**

| Difference between displayed and measured values | Action |
|---|---|
| 1.5°C or less | Im Seowoo records the room as passed |
| More than 1.5°C | Keep the sales hold in place, and Lee Doha re-inspects (mandatory) |

### 5-3. Resuming Sales
- Rooms that pass verification may resume sales from 16:00 on Wednesday, 12/16.
- Resuming sales on the same day is optional.
