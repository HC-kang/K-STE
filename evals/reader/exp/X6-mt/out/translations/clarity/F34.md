# [Notice] Changes to Walkie-Talkie Rental and Return Rules (Effective 12/07)

**Audience:** Venue guide staff and on-site supervisors, Ormit Performance
**Effective:** Starting shifts on 12/07 (Mon)

## 1. Reason for Change
Low walkie-talkie batteries and missed returns have delayed guide duties for subsequent performances. To prevent this, the rental and return procedures are changed as follows.

## 2. Exclusions
Staff of external rental companies use their own equipment, so these rental rules do not apply to them.

## 3. Key Changes
**All staff**
- Verbal rentals are discontinued. Both rentals and returns of walkie-talkies must be recorded in the "Mudae-On" system.

**Guide staff**
- **Pickup:** Collect from the 1st-floor equipment room at least 20 minutes before your shift starts, using your own employee ID card. Collection on someone else's behalf is prohibited.
- **Quantity:** One walkie-talkie per person is the standard.
- **Battery check:** When collecting, be sure the battery is at least 80%. If it is below 80%, request a replacement from the equipment manager and enter the reason for replacement in the "Mudae-On" rental record.
- **Return:** Return the unit to the charging station in the 1st-floor equipment room within 15 minutes after your shift ends, and mark the return as completed in "Mudae-On."
- **Consecutive shifts:** If performances are back-to-back and the gap between two shifts is 30 minutes or less, the on-site supervisor may approve continued use in the system. No interim return is required; return the unit within 15 minutes after the final shift ends.

**On-site supervisors**
- **Spare walkie-talkies:** Up to 2 additional units may be rented per performance.
- **Consecutive shift approval:** Approve continued use in the system for staff who meet the consecutive shift requirements above.
- **Unreturned check:** Within 40 minutes after the last performance of the day ends, check the list of unreturned units and send the list to equipment manager Ha Nu-gyeom.

## 4. Advance Action (All Staff)
By **16:00 on 12/04 (Fri)**, please check in "Mudae-On" that your employee ID number is correctly registered. If the number is different or cannot be verified, you may not be able to collect a walkie-talkie after 12/07, so be sure to check before the deadline.
