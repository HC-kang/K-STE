**Subject: [Notice] Cafeteria Closure and Alternative Lunchbox Application, Nov. 16 (Mon) to Nov. 20 (Fri)**

Dear Sandbit Foods employees,

The cafeteria will be closed during the period below due to drainage pipe replacement work. All production and office staff may apply for alternative lunchboxes on the days they are scheduled to work during the closure. Applying is not mandatory; please apply only if you need one.

**1. Cafeteria Closure and Reopening**
- Closure period: Nov. 16 (Mon) to Nov. 20 (Fri)
- Normal operations resume: Nov. 23 (Mon), 11:30

**2. Alternative Lunchbox Application**
- Eligible: All production and office staff working during the closure period
- How to apply: Select the dates you will use the service in the in-house app "Bapmaru"
- Application deadline: Nov. 12 (Thu), 16:00
- You must apply separately for each day you need a lunchbox.
- Quantity: One meal per person per working day. Applications cannot be made for days off.
- Menu: Choose one of two options, regular or vegetarian. You cannot apply for both at the same time.
- Employee contribution: 2,000 KRW per meal, regardless of menu

**3. Lunchbox Pickup**
- Location: Temporary serving station on the 1st floor of the factory
- Day shift workers: 11:30 to 12:30 on the day you applied for
- Night shift workers: 20:00 to 21:00 on the day you applied for

**4. Cancelling an Application**
- You can cancel in Bapmaru until Nov. 13 (Fri), 12:00.
- After this time, cancellations and refunds of the contribution are not possible.

**5. If Food Allergies Prevent Use of Either Menu**
- Report to the Welfare Team by Nov. 12 (Thu), 16:00 to receive meal expense support without applying for a lunchbox.
- Support amount: 5,000 KRW for each actual working day during the closure period
- Required documents: Submit a purchase receipt to Bapmaru by Nov. 23 (Mon), 12:00. Support will not be provided if the receipt is not submitted by the deadline.

**6. Inquiries**
- Welfare Team, Ha-rin Ko (ext. 462)
- Questions regarding lunchbox pickup and allergy reports are accepted.

We apologize for the inconvenience caused by the construction. Thank you for your cooperation.

Welfare Team
