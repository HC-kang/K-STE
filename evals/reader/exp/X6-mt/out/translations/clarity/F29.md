**Subject: [Sodam Chukgi] Free Roller Replacement Notice for Label Printer P26 (Registration Deadline: Thu 12/03, 16:00)**

Hello. This is the Service Team at Sodam Chukgi.

We have confirmed that the feed rollers in label printer P26 units with serial numbers **S6100 to S6199** may wear out quickly. As wear progresses, label printing position may become misaligned. We will replace the rollers free of charge for customers who own these units. Please review the information below and register by the deadline.

**1. Eligibility**
- Customers who own label printer P26 units with serial numbers S6100 to S6199

**2. Costs**
- Replacement labor and round-trip shipping are free of charge.
- If you want a substitute unit on loan during the replacement period, you may choose this option. The rental fee is 8,000 KRW per day.

**3. Replacement Registration (Required)**
- Registration deadline: **Thursday 12/03, until 16:00**
- How to register: Submit the following items under the "Roller Replacement" menu in the Service Portal.
  - Device serial number
  - Pickup address
  - Preferred pickup date
- Customers without a packing box may request a free packing box when registering.

**4. Pickup Schedule**
- Selectable dates: Monday 12/07, Tuesday 12/08, or Wednesday 12/09
- Pickup time: A courier designated by our company will visit between 09:00 and 13:00 on the date you choose.

**5. Shipping Instructions**
- Please send **only the printer body**. Keep the power adapter and remaining labels separate and store them at your site.
- **Cushioned packaging is mandatory** to prevent damage during transit. Using the original box is optional.

**6. Shipment of Repaired Units**
- We will ship repaired units within 3 business days, starting the business day after receipt. Business days are Monday through Friday.

**7. Customers Who Have Already Replaced the Same Roller**
- No replacement registration is required.
- However, you **must submit a work confirmation form** under "Replacement History" in the Service Portal **by Thursday 12/03, 16:00**.

**8. Contacts**
- Pickup and rental inquiries: Service Team, Baek Ideun / "Service Inquiry" in the Service Portal
- Work confirmation form inquiries: Repair Records Team, Na Haewon / "Records Inquiry" in the Service Portal

We apologize for the inconvenience and will process everything promptly and accurately. Thank you.

Sodam Chukgi Service Team
