# Packaging Material Trial Operation Meeting Minutes

**To:** Production Technology Team, Packaging Line 2 Team Leader
**Meeting Date & Time:** Tuesday, November 3, 2026, 10:00–10:30
**Location:** 2nd Floor Conference Room, Headquarters
**Agenda:** Finalize the packaging material trial operation plan

## 1. Background of the Trial
Due to an increase in reports of corner damage on products using the existing cushioning material, it was decided to trial a new cushioning material.

## 2. Trial Overview
| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Date & Time | Thursday, November 5, 09:00–12:00 |
| Location | Packaging Line 2, Plant 1 |
| Target | 240 boxes using the new cushioning material |
| Excluded | Packaging Line 1 (no need to receive new cushioning material or register trial results) |

## 3. Assigned Responsibilities
| Responsible Party | Task | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Yoon Se-ram (Production Technology) | Place 300 new cushioning materials at the material station for Packaging Line 2 | Wednesday, November 4, by 16:00 |
| Do Ha-jun (Line 2 Team Leader) | Check corner condition of all 240 trial boxes | During the trial |
| Do Ha-jun (Line 2 Team Leader) | Register inspection results in the "Packaging Results" system | Thursday, November 5, by 15:00 |

| Number of Corner Damages Found | Action |
|---|---|
| 5 boxes or more | **Immediately stop** the trial; Team Leader Do Ha-jun **reports by phone** to Yoon Se-ram |
| 4 boxes or fewer | Continue the trial until the planned 240 boxes are completed |

- Any additional trial beyond 240 boxes may only be conducted **after approval from Yoon Se-ram**.
