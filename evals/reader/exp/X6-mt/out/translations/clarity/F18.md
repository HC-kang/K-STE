# Online Performance Backup Line Switchover Runbook

**Bundle 4 | For On-Site Broadcast Engineers**

## 1. Overview

- **Background**: Due to maintenance on the primary line equipment, there is a possibility that the broadcast may be interrupted during the performance, so a backup line is being prepared.
- **Applicable performance**: 11/27 (Fri) 19:30–21:00
- **Work location**: 3rd floor broadcast room at headquarters, console "별송"

## 2. Roles

| Role | Assignee | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Broadcast engineer | Yoon Taeril | Test backup line connection, execute line switchover, broadcast standby screen, submit records |
| Audio engineer | Seo Gaon | Verify and record audio after switchover |
| On-site supervisor | Moon Haesol | Decide whether to halt the performance broadcast |

## 3. Pre-Performance Preparation (by 19:00)

1. Yoon Taeril completes the backup line connection test by 19:00.
2. Test video is checked **only via internal preview**. **Do not broadcast to the viewer channel.**
3. **Disable** the primary line auto-return function and keep it disabled throughout the performance.

## 4. Switchover Criteria During the Performance

The criteria are the primary line loss rate and its duration.

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Loss rate of 3% or higher maintained for **20 consecutive seconds** | **Switchover to backup line is mandatory.** No separate approval from Moon Haesol is required. |
| Loss rate below 3% | Do not switch. Internal preview check is permitted. |
| Loss rate 3% or higher but duration less than 20 seconds | Do not switch. Internal preview check is permitted. |

## 5. Post-Switchover Procedure

1. Yoon Taeril switches to the backup line.
2. Seo Gaon records the audio verification result in the 별송 work window **within 40 seconds** of the switchover.
3. If there is an audio problem, Seo Gaon **immediately** reports to Moon Haesol.

## 6. If the Backup Line Is Also Unstable

1. If the loss rate of 3% or higher is maintained for 20 consecutive seconds on the backup line, Yoon Taeril **broadcasts the standby screen**.
2. Only **Moon Haesol** decides whether to halt the performance broadcast. Yoon Taeril and Seo Gaon do not make the decision to halt.

## 7. Prohibition on Returning to the Primary Line

- Even if the primary line appears normal, **do not manually switch back before the performance ends.**
- The auto-return function must remain disabled throughout the performance.

## 8. Post-Performance Submission (by 21:20)

- Yoon Taeril submits the **switchover time and loss rate records** by 21:20.
- Even if no switchover occurred, submit as **"No switchover."**
