**[Outage Notice] Visitor QR Issuance Suspended in Headquarters Visitor System "MunPass" (Thu 12/17, from 08:50)**

Target: Staff who schedule headquarters visits, and 1st-floor reception desk staff

**1. Situation**
- Starting at 08:50 on Thursday, 12/17, visitor QR code issuance in "MunPass" was suspended.
- The cause is the expiration of the API key for the text message sending integration.
- The recovery owner is Ms. Sodam Lee of the Facilities IT Team, and the recovery target is 13:00 on Thursday, 12/17.
- Whether recovery has actually occurred will be announced in the #본사-방문안내 (HQ Visit Guidance) channel.

**2. Scope of Impact**
- Employee access cards are operating normally.
- Visitor QRs issued before the outage can still be used within their validity period. In this case, no paper visitor pass is needed.

**3. Actions for Scheduling Staff**

*For visitors scheduled between 09:00 and 12:00 on Thursday, 12/17 who do not have a valid QR, submitting a temporary visitor list is mandatory.*
- Enter the visitor's name, visit time, and the host employee's extension in the internal form "1217 방문대체" (1217 Visit Substitute).
- Submission deadline: 09:20 on Thursday, 12/17
- For visit appointments before 12:00 that are added after 09:20, do not use the form. Instead, register by phone with the reception desk (extension 107). Registration must be made at least 20 minutes before the scheduled visit time.

*For visit appointments after 12:00*
- A temporary visitor list is not required at this time.
- Please proceed after checking the operations team's follow-up notice posted at 11:30 on Thursday, 12/17.

**4. Visitor Entry and Escort Procedures**
- Visitors without a valid QR will receive a paper visitor pass at the 1st-floor reception desk of headquarters. Passes cannot be issued in the 3rd-floor reception room.
- Visitors with paper passes must be met and escorted by the host employee directly on the 1st floor.
- Visitors using a valid QR do not require additional escort due to this outage.

**5. Actions for Reception Desk Staff**
- Issue one paper visitor pass per visitor. Passes are valid until 18:00 on the same day.
- Be sure to collect the pass from each visitor when they leave.
- Report the number of paper passes issued and returned to Ms. Haeon Choi of the General Affairs Team by 18:20 on Thursday, 12/17.

**6. Inquiries**
- Phone registration for visitors and pass inquiries: Reception desk, extension 107
- QR system error inquiries: Ms. Sodam Lee, extension 733
