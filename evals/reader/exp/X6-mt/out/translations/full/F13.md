# Return Equipment Intake Procedure Meeting Minutes

Due to overlapping routes for return and outbound equipment, loading delays occurred. The intake procedure for return equipment at the Seobu Logistics Center of Daeonbit Rental has been finalized. The new procedure applies starting Wednesday, 11/18, to portable cooling fans returned to the Seobu Logistics Center. Returns to the Dongbu Logistics Center are excluded.

## Meeting Information

- Date and time: Monday, 11/16/2026, 08:30–09:00
- Location: Training Room, Seobu Logistics Center
- Agenda: Finalize the intake procedure for return equipment

## Decisions

### Advance Registration and Reservation

- Collection drivers must register their vehicle number and the number of units to be returned in the "Dolaeonbit" system by 16:00 the day before their visit.
- The scheduled return time is 10:00–12:00.
- The maximum number of cooling fans per vehicle is 8.
- The default unloading location is Dock No. 3 at the Seobu Logistics Center.

### When Return Quantity Exceeds 8 Units

Collection drivers must split the load across vehicles and make a separate reservation for each vehicle. Excess units must not be returned on a single vehicle.

### Equipment with Signs of Water Leakage

Collection drivers must contact intake manager Seo Nagyeom before unloading. This equipment must be unloaded at Quarantine Zone Q instead of Dock No. 3.

### Same-Day Urgent Returns

Returns without prior system registration are permitted only with prior approval from dispatch manager Ko Sion. Per-vehicle quantity limits must still be observed in this case.

## Action Items

- Seo Nagyeom: On the day of return, take two exterior photos of each piece of equipment and attach them to the system by 17:00.
- Procedure demonstration: To be held at Dock No. 3 on Tuesday, 11/17, 15:00–15:30. Intake staff must attend. Collection drivers do not need to attend.
