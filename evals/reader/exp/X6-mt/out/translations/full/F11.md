# Packaging Material Trial Operation Meeting Minutes

At the meeting held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, from 10:00 to 10:30 in the 2nd floor conference room at headquarters, the packaging material trial operation plan was finalized. The reason for the trial is the increase in reports of corner damage on products packed with the existing cushioning material. The trial will be conducted on Thursday, November 5, from 09:00 to 12:00, on Packaging Line 2 at Factory 1.

## Trial Scope

- Date and time: Thursday, November 5, 09:00–12:00
- Location: Packaging Line 2, Factory 1
- Target: 240 boxes using the new cushioning material

## Responsibilities by Role

- Production Technology Manager Yoon Se-ram must place 300 units of the new cushioning material at the material station on Packaging Line 2 by Wednesday, November 4, 16:00.
- Line 2 Team Leader Do Ha-jun must inspect the corner condition of all 240 trial boxes.
- Team Leader Do Ha-jun must register the corner condition inspection results in the "Packaging Results" system by Thursday, November 5, 15:00.

## Criteria for Stopping and Continuing

- If 5 or more boxes with corner damage are found, the trial must be stopped immediately. In this case, Team Leader Do Ha-jun must report to Manager Yoon Se-ram by phone.
- If 4 or fewer boxes have corner damage, the planned 240-box trial must continue.
- Additional trials may proceed only after approval by Manager Yoon Se-ram.

## Packaging Line 1 and Observation

- Packaging Line 1 is excluded from this trial. Packaging Line 1 does not need to receive the new cushioning material and does not need to register trial results.
- The Production Technology team may observe the trial on Thursday, November 5, from 09:00 to 10:00. Observation is optional.

## Schedule

|---|---|---|
