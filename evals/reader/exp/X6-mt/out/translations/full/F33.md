Serneun Design will change the per-trip support limit for customer-visit taxi fares from 35,000 KRW to 28,000 KRW, effective for trips taken on 12/01 (Tue). The change is being made because customer-visit transportation claims have been increasingly rejected for missing project codes.

The change applies to customer-visit transportation costs for field designers and project leads. Commuting costs are not covered by these rules.

- Fare claimants must register the receipt and project code under the "Customer Visit" menu in the "Bill-it" system. The deadline is 18:00 on the next working day after the trip.
- Project leads must complete approval by 15:00 on the next working day after registration.
- Approval of a project lead's own trips must be completed by the same deadline by Operations Team Lead Do Yeo-ul.

For visits that involve transporting equipment at a customer's request, up to 45,000 KRW per trip can be supported. In these cases, the system approval of Operations Team Lead Do Yeo-ul must be obtained before boarding the taxi. To apply this exception, the customer's equipment transport request email must also be attached. Any amount exceeding the limit without prior approval must be paid by the employee personally.

A single receipt must not be split into multiple claims. Fellow passengers do not need to be claimed separately; only the person who actually paid may submit the claim.

Transportation costs for trips taken through 11/30 (Mon) are subject to the existing limit. These costs must be registered by 18:00 on 12/03 (Thu), and no revision to match the new limit is required.

Affected employees must set a default project code in their "Bill-it" profile by 17:00 on 11/27 (Fri). For questions, contact Finance Support's Min Sae-ron via the messenger channel "교통비도움" (Transportation Help).

To be confirmed: the amount exceeding the limit without prior approval. It may refer to the amount above the 28,000 KRW limit, or to the amount above the 45,000 KRW limit under the equipment transport exception.
