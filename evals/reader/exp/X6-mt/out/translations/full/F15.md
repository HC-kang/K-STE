# Room Temperature Sensor Replacement Meeting Minutes

Following repeated discrepancies between the displayed temperature and the actual measured temperature in 7th-floor guest rooms, this meeting finalized the replacement sequence for the room temperature sensors. The target rooms are 12 rooms on the 7th floor, Rooms 701 through 712.

## Meeting Overview

- Date and time: Monday, December 14, 2026, 09:00–09:30
- Location: Hotel basement operations meeting room
- Agenda: Finalize the room temperature sensor replacement sequence

## Replacement Targets

- 7th floor, Rooms 701–712, 12 rooms in total
- 8th floor rooms are excluded from this work. No access preparation is required for 8th floor rooms.

## Decisions

### Work Sequence

1. Start with vacant rooms as of Wednesday, December 16, 10:00.
2. Occupied rooms may be worked on only if the guest has consented to entry.
3. Rooms without guest entry consent are excluded from today's work.
4. Lee Do-ha uses one new sensor per room.

### Verification and Resumption of Sales

- Im Seo-woo must verify each room with a sensor replacement for 20 minutes using a portable meter.
- Rooms must not be returned to sale before verification.
- If the difference between the displayed and measured values is 1.5 degrees or less, Im Seo-woo records the room as passed.
- If the difference exceeds 1.5 degrees, the sales hold remains in place. In this case, Lee Do-ha must re-inspect the room.
- Jo Hae-rin may resume sales for rooms that passed verification starting Wednesday, December 16, 16:00. Sales do not have to resume on that day.
