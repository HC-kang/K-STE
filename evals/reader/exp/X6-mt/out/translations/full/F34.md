Starting with the shift on 12/07 (Mon), the walkie-talkie rental and return rules at the Ormit Concert venue will change. Delays in the next performance guidance work caused by low walkie-talkie batteries and missed returns have prompted the change. The rules apply to venue guide staff and on-site managers. External venue rental company staff use their own equipment, so this rental policy does not apply to them.

Verbal rentals are discontinued. All rentals and returns must be recorded in the "MuDae-On" system.

- Guide staff must receive a walkie-talkie from the 1st-floor equipment room using their own employee ID card at least 20 minutes before the start of their shift. Proxy pickup is not allowed.
- The default rental is 1 walkie-talkie per person. On-site managers may rent 2 additional spare walkie-talkies per performance.
- When receiving a walkie-talkie, check that the battery is at least 80%. If it is below 80%, request a replacement from the equipment staff and enter the reason for the replacement in the rental record.
- Guide staff must return the walkie-talkie to the 1st-floor equipment room charging station within 15 minutes after the end of their shift, and complete the return in "MuDae-On."
- Staff working consecutive performances with 30 minutes or less between shifts may keep using the walkie-talkie if the on-site manager approves it in the system. In this case, they do not need to return it in between, but must return it within 15 minutes after the end of their last shift.
- On-site managers must check the list of unreturned items within 40 minutes after the last performance of the day ends. The checked list must be sent to equipment manager Ha Nu-gyeom.

All target staff must confirm their employee ID number in "MuDae-On" by 12/04 (Fri) 16:00. For inquiries, contact equipment manager Ha Nu-gyeom (ext. 573).
