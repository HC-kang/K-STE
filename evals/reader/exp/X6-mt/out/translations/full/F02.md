The cafeteria will be closed from November 16 (Mon) to November 20 (Fri) because the cafeteria's drain pipes are being replaced. Regular cafeteria operations will resume on November 23 (Mon) at 11:30.

During the cafeteria closure period, all production and office staff who come to work may request a replacement lunchbox. Requesting a lunchbox is optional.

Employees who need a lunchbox must submit a request for each date of use. Select the dates you want to use in the in-house app "Bapmaru" by November 12 (Thu) at 16:00. The request conditions are as follows:

- Quantity: One meal per employee for each workday. Lunchboxes cannot be requested on days off.
- Type: You must choose one type, either regular or vegetarian. You cannot request both types at the same time.
- Employee contribution: 2,000 KRW per meal, regardless of type.

Lunchboxes will be picked up on the day of the request at the temporary serving counter on the 1st floor of the factory. Day-shift workers pick up between 11:30 and 12:30, and night-shift workers pick up between 20:00 and 21:00.

Requests can be canceled in Bapmaru until November 13 (Fri) at 12:00. After that, requests cannot be canceled, and the employee contribution cannot be refunded.

Employees who cannot eat either the regular or vegetarian menu due to a food allergy must report it to the Welfare Team by November 12 (Thu) at 16:00. If you report, you can receive meal allowance support instead of requesting a lunchbox. The meal allowance support is 5,000 KRW for each actual workday during the cafeteria closure period. You must submit a purchase receipt to Bapmaru by November 23 (Mon) at 12:00.

For questions about lunchbox pickup and allergy reporting, contact Ha-rin Ko of the Welfare Team (extension 462).
