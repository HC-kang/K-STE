The storage period policy takes effect on 11/23 (Mon). From that day, the default storage period for newly received actual analysis samples will be reduced from 60 days to 30 days. This change addresses the problem of samples past their storage deadline continuing to occupy freezer space.

This applies to researchers and sample management staff in the MoraFit Research Analysis Lab. Educational model samples are not covered. Samples received before 11/23 (Mon) keep their existing 60-day storage period, and they do not need to be re-registered under the new policy.

The changed procedures are as follows:

- **Sample registration:** The receiving researcher must register new samples in '시료뜰' under 'Intake Registration' by 17:00 on the day of receipt.
- **Label attachment:** Sample management staff must print the system label and attach it to the sample container by 12:00 on the next working day after registration.
- **Storage extension:** If a storage extension is needed, the receiving researcher must apply in '시료뜰' by 17:00 three days before the expiration date. The extension requires approval from the Lab Director, 연보민.
- **Extension limit:** An extension may be granted once per sample, for a maximum of 15 days. Storage periods must not be extended without approval.
- **Moving expired samples:** Sample management staff must move expired samples to the Disposal Holding Area on the 2nd floor by 16:00 on the next working day after expiration. Researchers do not need to move them directly.

Researchers and sample management staff must complete the check-off confirming they have reviewed the revised policy in '시료뜰' by 17:00 on 11/20 (Fri). For inquiries, contact Research Support, 배소운 (ext. 462).
