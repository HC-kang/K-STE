# Haesol Documentation Center B-Archive Humidity Alarm Response Runbook

Haesol Documentation Center may experience a rise in humidity in the archive room while the dehumidifier drain pipe is being replaced, so temporary response procedures will apply. This runbook summarizes the procedures that facility duty staff follow when responding to a humidity alarm.

## Scope

- Application period: Tuesday, December 15, 08:00 through Thursday, December 17, 20:00
- Applies to: B-Archive Room on B1 (basement level 1) only
- A-Archive Room: existing procedures remain in effect.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Facility duty staff Im Nagyeom | Inspects the site and operates the portable dehumidifier. |
| Preservation staff Cha Subin | Decides on document relocation. |
| Facility manager Ryu Dohyun | Provides equipment support. |

## Alarm Response Procedure

1. When the monitoring screen "Bogonun" shows relative humidity at 60% or above for 10 consecutive minutes, begin the alarm response.
2. The duty staff must arrive on site within 5 minutes after the alarm response begins.
3. On site, measure humidity with a handheld meter.
4. If the handheld meter reading is 58% or above, the portable dehumidifier must be operated.
5. If the handheld meter reading is below 58%, the portable dehumidifier does not need to be operated. In this case, request that Ryu Dohyun inspect the monitoring sensor.
6. If the dehumidifier was operated, measure again with the handheld meter after 20 minutes.
7. If the re-measurement is 58% or above, request that Cha Subin decide on document relocation.
8. If the re-measurement is below 58%, the dehumidifier may be turned off. It may remain running, but it must be turned off before the end of the duty shift.

**Duty staff must not relocate documents on their own.**

## Conditions for Using the Portable Dehumidifier

- Must use a dedicated outlet for the B-Archive Room.
- No more than 2 units may operate at the same time.
- Must not use hallway outlets or extension cords.

## Records

- The alarm occurrence time, on-site measurement values, and actions taken on site must be recorded in "Bogonun" within 40 minutes after the alarm response begins.
- Records must be made even if the dehumidifier was not operated.
- Shifts without an alarm do not need a separate alarm response record.
