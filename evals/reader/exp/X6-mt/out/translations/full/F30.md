Due to the ceiling leak repair at Solmaru Hall, all events booked at Solmaru Hall from 12/14 (Mon) through 12/18 (Fri) will be moved to Byeolmaru Hall, located on the 2nd floor of the same building. Booked session times and venue fees remain unchanged.

All affected companies must select either agreement to the move or cancellation via the reservation portal's "Event Confirmation" by 12/08 (Tue) 15:00. If you select cancellation by 12/08 (Tue) 15:00, the full deposit will be refunded. Cancellations after that point will be subject to the cancellation fees under the existing contract.

The maximum capacity of Byeolmaru Hall is 60 people. Submit the final attendance count and seating arrangement via "Event Confirmation" by 12/10 (Thu) 12:00. Companies whose final attendance exceeds 60 must request separate space arrangements from the Reservation Operations Team by 12/08 (Tue) 15:00. Agreeing to the move alone does not confirm a space.

If the existing reservation included wireless microphones, the same quantity as originally requested will be provided. Equipment already requested does not need to be requested again. Additional microphones can be requested optionally at 20,000 KRW per unit. To request additional microphones, use "Add Equipment" on the reservation portal by 12/10 (Thu) 12:00.

Entry for event preparation is free from 30 minutes before the reservation start time. Early entry beyond that is not permitted. Please complete teardown by the reservation end time.

For space and cancellation inquiries, contact Ms. Seo Ga-on of the Reservation Operations Team via "Reservation Inquiry" on the reservation portal. For equipment inquiries, contact Ms. Oh Rubin of the On-site Support Team via "Equipment Inquiry" on the reservation portal.
