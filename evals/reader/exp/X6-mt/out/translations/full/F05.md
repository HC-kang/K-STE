Byeolgori Research Institute will conduct a shared-equipment asset audit in November to resolve mismatches between its equipment management ledger and the actual storage locations of equipment.

The audit covers laptops, tablets, and measuring instruments that carry company asset numbers. Personally owned equipment and consumables without asset numbers are excluded.

All research and administrative staff must review the list of equipment assigned to them in the virtual asset system "Byeoljangbu" and mark it "Confirmed" by 17:00 on Tuesday, 11/24. Staff with no assigned equipment must also mark "No assignment" by the same deadline. No separate email report is required.

Equipment holders must enter each item's asset number and current location in Byeoljangbu and attach one photo showing the asset number. The next steps depend on the equipment's condition.

- Working equipment: Online confirmation in Byeoljangbu is sufficient. You do not need to bring it to the audit room.
- Malfunctioning equipment: In addition to online confirmation, return it to the audit room on the 1st floor of the Research Building on Wednesday, 11/25, from 10:00 to 16:00.
- Equipment sent to an external repair vendor: No direct return is required. Attach the repair receipt in Byeoljangbu by 17:00 on Tuesday, 11/24. The receipt may be submitted in place of the asset number photo.

If a single staff member has three or more assigned items, the team leader must perform an additional check. The team leader must approve in Byeoljangbu by 12:00 on Thursday, 11/26.

Any unregistered equipment discovered during the audit must be reported to the Asset Team, regardless of whether it has an asset number. Staff must not dispose of it or transfer it to others on their own.

An online entry briefing will be held on Friday, 11/20, from 15:00 to 15:30 in the internal video conference room "Byeolgyosil." Attendance is optional, but the audit obligation remains in effect for those who do not attend.

For questions about assignment lists and repair receipts, contact Do Seorin, Asset Team (ext. 643). For adjusting return times for malfunctioning equipment, contact Cha Yugeon, Facilities Team (ext. 297).
