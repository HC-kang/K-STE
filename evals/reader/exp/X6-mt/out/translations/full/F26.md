Due to floor repairs at the East Refrigerated Center, the delivery time for shipments from November 12 (Thu) through November 14 (Sat) will be changed from the existing 07:00–09:00 to 10:00–12:00.

All food store owners shipping from the East Refrigerated Center must submit their receiving availability through "Delivery Change Confirmation" on the customer portal by 18:00 on November 10 (Tue). Stores that cannot receive deliveries between 10:00 and 12:00 must select pickup at a nearby partner cold storage facility on the same submission screen. Stores that select cold storage will pick up their orders on the delivery day from 13:00 to 17:00. Storage fees will be borne by Nuri Gyeol Temperature Logistics.

Shipments from the West Refrigerated Center will keep their existing delivery times, so there is no need to submit a Delivery Change Confirmation.

The 40 kg per-order delivery weight limit remains in effect during the change period, and there are no additional delivery fees. Order quantities can be changed on the existing ordering screen. Starting with shipments on November 16 (Mon), the regular 07:00–09:00 delivery schedule will resume.

For questions about selecting a pickup location, contact Delivery Operations Team member Ko Se-rin or use "Delivery Inquiry" on the customer portal.

Delivery Operations Team, Nuri Gyeol Temperature Logistics
