Starting at 20:00 on Monday, 11/16, the night emergency shipment approval rules at Nubeon Logistics will change. The existing phone approval will be abolished, and approval must be obtained from the shift leader through the "Emergency Shipment" menu in the Jimgyeol system.

The reason for the change is that approval records for night emergency shipments have repeatedly been missing. The change applies to night shipment staff and shipment shift leaders. The day shipment team will keep the existing rules. The new rules apply to emergency shipment requests received at or after 20:00 on Monday, 11/16.

The changes are as follows:

- Staff must submit approval requests at least 40 minutes before the scheduled vehicle departure time.
- Shift leaders must complete approval at least 15 minutes before the scheduled vehicle departure time.
- A single request must cover 30 boxes or fewer. Requests for more than 30 boxes must be split into multiple requests of 30 or fewer, each requiring its own approval.
- Paper emergency shipment forms may be used only during a system failure. In that case, the shift leader's signature must be obtained before shipment.
- If a paper emergency shipment form was used, staff must enter the record within 2 hours after the system is restored.
- If only the vehicle number changes for an already approved request, staff may correct the number without requesting re-approval.

Both staff and shift leaders must watch the 12-minute video in the Jimgyeol training menu and click the confirmation button by 18:00 on Friday, 11/13.

For questions, contact Heo Da-on in Operations Support through the internal messenger "야간출고문의" (Night Shipment Inquiry). Report system failures through the same channel.
