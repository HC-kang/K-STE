# Cold Raw Material Receiving Hold Runbook

Serion Food discovered a missed calibration on the vehicle thermometers, so the refrigerated raw materials delivered by designated vehicles are being given an additional inspection. This runbook defines the measurement, judgment, hold, and approval procedures to be followed by receiving inspection staff at the Eastern Distribution Center.

## Scope

- Period of application: Monday 11/16 through Wednesday 11/18
- Location: Dock 2, Eastern Distribution Center
- Target vehicles: Vehicles arriving at Dock 2 whose license plate ends in 62 or 84

Vehicles outside these criteria continue under the existing inspection process. For these vehicles, no additional 3-box measurement or additional hold registration is required.

## Roles

| Personnel | Role |
|---|---|
| Receiving inspector Jio Han | Measures the core temperature of raw material boxes. |
| Quality duty officer Dabin Jung | Decides whether held raw materials are usable. |
| Warehouse supervisor Taegeon Yoo | Assigns storage locations for held raw materials. |

## Judgment Criteria

| Condition | Judgment |
|---|---|
| All three measured values are between 0.0°C and 5.0°C inclusive, and there is no damage to the box seals. | Receive normally. |
| At least one measured value is outside the 0.0°C to 5.0°C range. | Hold the entire raw material load from that vehicle. |
| All measured values are within range, but box seal damage is found. | Hold the entire raw material load from that vehicle. |

Raw materials held due to seal damage must not be released from hold by re-measurement alone.

## Procedure

1. When a vehicle arrives at Dock 2, check whether the license plate ends in 62 or 84.
2. If it is a target vehicle, measure the core temperature of 3 raw material boxes within 15 minutes of arrival.
3. Record all three measured values.
4. Decide whether to receive or hold according to the judgment criteria.
5. If the decision is to hold, move the raw materials to refrigerator Zone Q.
6. Attach a yellow hold tag to the held raw materials.
7. Register the hold in the inventory system "Chango" within 30 minutes of vehicle arrival. On-site photos may be attached.
8. Dabin Jung must record an approval or return decision in Chango within 60 minutes of hold registration.

Held raw materials must not be released to production before Dabin Jung approves them.
