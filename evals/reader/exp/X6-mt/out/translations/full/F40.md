The existing inventory count on 12/22 (Tue) is canceled due to increased year-end shipments, and will be rescheduled to times that do not overlap with shipping work. This is the year-end fabric inventory count by warehouse, led by Bae Su-an of the Finance Management Team at Goun-maru Textile. The candidate sessions are the two below, and one session will be assigned to each warehouse.

- 12/23 (Wed) 08:00–10:00
- 12/24 (Thu) 15:00–17:00

Inventory managers must register all available candidate sessions in the inventory management system by 12/18 (Fri) 16:00. If both candidate sessions overlap with shipping work, submit your shipping schedule to Bae Su-an by email by the same deadline. In this case, you do not need to register any candidate sessions.

Bae Su-an will send the confirmed session for each warehouse by 12/21 (Mon) 10:00.

The inventory manager is expected to attend in person. Only if the manager is on leave may a substitute from the same warehouse be designated. The substitute's name must be reported to Bae Su-an by 12/21 (Mon) 12:00.

The count is conducted at each assigned warehouse; there is no need to visit headquarters.

Counting procedure:
- Inventory movement at the assigned warehouse must stop from 30 minutes before the start of the assigned session until its end. Inventory may be moved outside the session times.
- The counting unit is the number of fabric rolls. Packaging does not need to be opened.
- Rolls whose barcodes cannot be read may be recorded manually.
- The results sheet must be uploaded to the inventory management system by 18:00 on the day of the count.

For schedule questions, contact Bae Su-an (ext. 752). For system errors, contact Jeon Yu-dam (ext. 759).
