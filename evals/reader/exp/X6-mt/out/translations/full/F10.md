On 12/17 (Thu), visitor QR code issuance for the headquarters visitor system "Munpass" was suspended starting at 08:50. The cause is the expiration of the API key for the SMS sending integration. Lee So-dam of the Facilities IT Team is handling the recovery, with a target recovery time of 13:00 on 12/17 (Thu). Actual recovery updates will be posted in the #본사-방문안내 channel.

Employee access cards work as usual. Visitor QR codes issued before the outage can still be used within their validity period, and in that case paper visitor passes do not need to be issued.

Reception staff should do the following:
- If any visitors scheduled between 09:00 and 12:00 on 12/17 (Thu) do not have a valid QR code, you must submit a temporary list. Enter each visitor's name, visit time, and the host employee's extension in the company form "1217 방문대체" by 09:20 on 12/17 (Thu).
- Visit reservations for before 12:00 that are added after 09:20 should not be entered in the form. Instead, you must register them by phone with the front desk. Register at least 20 minutes before the scheduled visit time.
- Visitors who receive a paper pass must be met on the 1st floor by the host employee, who must escort them. Visitors using a valid QR code do not need any additional escort due to this outage.
- For visit reservations after 12:00, read the follow-up notice from the operations team, which will be posted at 11:30 on 12/17 (Thu), before proceeding. You do not need to submit a temporary list for these reservations at this time.

Front desk staff should do the following:
- Issue paper visitor passes at the front desk on the 1st floor of headquarters to visitors who do not have a valid QR code. Passes cannot be issued at the 3rd-floor reception room.
- Issue one paper pass per visitor. Passes are valid until 18:00 on the same day.
- Be sure to collect the paper pass when each visitor leaves.
- Report the number of paper passes issued and returned to Choi Hae-on of the General Affairs Team by 18:20 on 12/17 (Thu).

For visitor phone registrations and pass inquiries, call the front desk at extension 107. For QR system error inquiries, call Lee So-dam at extension 733.
