Due to the year-end accounting system migration, we will close December business expenses early. Personal expenses are excluded from reimbursement.

Business expenses paid from 12/01 (Tue) to 12/18 (Fri) must be submitted by the employee who incurred them to the virtual system "Sembagune" by 12/21 (Mon) 15:00. The submission location is Sembagune > Expense Settlement > December Early Close, and it is the same for headquarters and branch offices.

- For each submission, you must enter the receipt and the business purpose.
- For paper receipts, you may attach a photo. You do not need to submit the original separately.
- For each expense of 300,000 KRW or more, you must attach a team leader's prior approval record in addition to the general submission materials. This includes expenses of exactly 300,000 KRW.

Business expenses scheduled to be paid from 12/19 (Sat) to 12/31 (Thu) must be pre-registered in the "Exception Expenses" menu of Sembagune by 12/18 (Fri) 12:00. After actually paying for pre-registered exception expenses, you must submit the receipt and business purpose by 12/31 (Thu) 17:00. If a team leader's prior approval record applies, this record must also be submitted.

Employees with no business expenses in December do not need to report 0 KRW, and they do not need to submit a separate certification.

Team leaders must approve or reject general expenses by 12/22 (Tue) 12:00, and exception expenses by 12/31 (Thu) 18:00.

General expenses approved by 12/22 (Tue) 12:00 will be paid on 12/24 (Thu). Exception expenses are not eligible for this 12/24 (Thu) payment.
