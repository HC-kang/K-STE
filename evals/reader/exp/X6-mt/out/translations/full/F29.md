Sodam Chukgi will replace the feed rollers free of charge for label printers P26 with serial numbers S6100 to S6199. The target units may experience label position errors because their feed rollers wear out quickly.

Target companies must register the serial number, pickup address, and preferred pickup date through the service portal, "Roller Replacement," by 16:00 on 12/03 (Thu). Choose a preferred pickup date from 12/07 (Mon) to 12/09 (Wed). Our designated courier will visit between 09:00 and 13:00 on the selected day.

Shipping contents and packaging:
- Send only the printer unit.
- Separate the power adapter and remaining labels and keep them at your company.
- Packaging must include cushioning.
- Using the original box is optional.
- Companies without a box can request a free packaging box when registering for replacement.

Replacement labor and round-trip shipping are free. Loaner equipment rental is optional, at 8,000 KRW per day.

Companies that have already replaced the same roller do not need to submit a replacement request. However, they must submit a work confirmation certificate through the portal's "Replacement History" by 16:00 on 12/03 (Thu).

We will ship repaired units within 3 business days, starting from the business day after the receipt date. Business days are Monday through Friday.

Send inquiries about pickup and rental to Baek Ideun of the Service Team through the portal's "Service Inquiry." Send inquiries about issuing work confirmation certificates to Na Haewon of the Repair Records Team through the portal's "Records Inquiry."

To confirm: within 3 business days from the business day after the receipt date. This may be read as counting the business day after the receipt date as Day 1, or as counting starting from the day after that.
