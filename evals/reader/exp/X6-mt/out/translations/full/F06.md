On 11/12 (Thu), starting at 09:10, the shipping label printing system "Taekpin" in the 2nd-floor packing area of the Dongbu Center went down. The cause was a missed renewal of the shipping label server certificate. The carrier delivery tracking function is operating normally.

Recovery is handled by Seo Doyun of the IT Operations Team. The recovery target is 11/12 (Thu) 11:30. Whether recovery has actually been completed will be announced separately in the #동부센터-장애 channel.

### Shipping Staff

- Do not use the Taekpin reprint button after the outage.
- Do not re-submit the same order until the recovery announcement.
- Handwritten shipping labels may be used only for urgent orders to be loaded on the 12:00 departure truck today.
- Regular orders must be stored on the 2nd-floor hold shelf after packing is complete.
- Handwritten shipping labels must be received from floor supervisor Kang Hari. An initial 15 sheets are issued per shipping staff member. Additional sheets require approval from Supervisor Kang Hari.
- Staff who used handwritten labels must enter the order number, label number, and number of sheets used in the shared document "11월 수기출고" by 11/12 (Thu) 10:50.
- Labels that were printed before the outage and whose barcodes scan correctly may still be used. Orders for these labels do not need to be entered in the shared document.
