Due to the end of support for the existing authentication equipment, we will switch work account authentication to the virtual system "Gyeolzamgeum" at 09:00 on 11/13 (Fri).

The target is all regular and contract employees, who must register Gyeolzamgeum. External visitors without personal work accounts are excluded.

The registration period is from 09:00 on 11/09 (Mon) to 18:00 on 11/12 (Thu). Register using your own work account via Company Portal > My Account > Gyeolzamgeum Registration. You must choose one registration method: a personal mobile phone app or a company security key. You do not have to register both.

Employees who do not want to use a personal mobile phone must apply for a security key through the portal by 12:00 on 11/10 (Tue). One security key per person is provided free of charge. Pickup date/time and location: 13:00-17:00 on 11/11 (Wed), Support Office on the 2nd floor of headquarters. Bring your employee ID card when picking up.

Employees on leave for the entire registration period are exempt from this registration. These employees must register Gyeolzamgeum by 17:00 on the day they return to work.

The new authentication takes effect at 09:00 on 11/13 (Fri). Employees who have not registered by then will not be able to log in to their work accounts until they complete registration.

If you encounter errors during registration, contact Yu Da-on of the Information Security Team via company messenger. For security key pickup schedule inquiries, contact Bae So-yul of the General Affairs Team via company messenger.
